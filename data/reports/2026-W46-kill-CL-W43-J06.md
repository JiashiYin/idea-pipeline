Glossary (each term once): **non-rigid** — cloth, cables and rods, liquids, granular material, volumetric soft bodies; everything a
rigid-body engine does not step. **Suite** — a fixed set of tasks with a shared interface and success criteria on which policies are
scored. **Porting / migration** — re-implementing a task written for one simulator in another, keeping its assets and success
check. **PBD / VBD / MPM / SPH / FEM** — position-based dynamics, vertex block descent, the material point method, smoothed-particle
hydrodynamics and the finite element method: the solver families used for cloth and soft solids (PBD, VBD, FEM), pastes and sand
(MPM) and liquids (SPH). **Coupling** — exchanging forces between two solvers inside one time step. **Health grade** — a per-world
label (Alloy: OK / DEGRADED / INVALID) that says whether the physics, not the policy, failed. **Agentic learning system** — a coding
agent that writes a control program from demonstrations and a few environment trials, benchmarked beside learned policies.

## 3. The two closest papers' limitation text (verbatim, grep-verified)

**RoboVerse 2504.18904, sec. VII Limitations.** "While RoboVerse provides a comprehensive and scalable platform, several limitations
remain. First, the integration of a unified format for non-rigid objects is not yet fully supported, which we leave for future work
to develop." **Sec. IV-B:** "The main source of these data is migration from existing simulation environments." **App. XI-L
(GarmentLab):** "we directly roll out the trajectories provided by the official codebase in Isaac Sim ... Although the trajectory
provided by the official codebase is limited and hard-coded, we further extend the number of demonstrations by applying different
garments and textures, and all the demonstrations are validated by the original success checker. Finally, we have successfully
collected 6k trajectories." **App. XIV-C:** "parameters such as friction and mass are inherently difficult to estimate purely from
visual data, introducing uncertainties that may affect simulation fidelity."

**Alloy 2611.16312, sec. 5 Scope.** "Alloy is an early, 0.x release, and its interfaces may still change. What does not work yet is
listed on one public page, each item with its measured size and what to do instead." **That page (`docs/keep-in-mind/`, fetched
2026-11-15), cloth:** "A woven fabric card built from published bending data drapes differently from the same fabric on a drape
tester, even when converged." "Sheets dropped onto each other can end with their layers crossed, and health does not flag it."
**Soft bodies:** "A body dropped on a soft solid bounces back almost as fast as it arrived, far more than the materials' restitution
says." **Liquids:** "A pour alone can splash a few percent over its container's rim (3 % of a 6 ml pour), and health stays OK."
**Sec. 2:** "Alloy does not replace these solvers; it is built on them." **Sec. 3.6:** the package ships "nine ready-made
environments, legged-locomotion environments, 90 material cards and 118 object cards. A further library of 5,423 object cards can be
downloaded." No sentence in the paper ports or names an incumbent suite's task; incumbents appear in sec. 2 only as engines
(Genesis "combines multiple solvers for robotics in one platform").

**Third, ManiLadder 2611.16374, sec. 3 and 4.** "1D ropes are represented as articulated chains of capsule-shaped rigid bodies, 2D
cloths are simulated with a PBD solver, and 3D deformable materials are modeled with solvers such as MPM for clay-like objects and
SPH for liquids." "Although the aforementioned calibration process is objective, it remains subject to limitations regarding the
number of evaluation trials and the specific policy employed." The paper names no physical validation of its deformable solvers; its
real-world check is of the difficulty ordering on eight tasks, not of material behaviour.

## 4. Why has this not been done

It has been done, in three pieces, each with a date. The port-and-synthesise platform: RoboVerse, 2025-04-26, for rigid and
articulated tasks with GarmentLab as its one migrated non-rigid suite, and the non-rigid format declared future work. The organised
non-rigid suite scored on policies and agents: ManiLadder, 2026-11-12, forty-plus deformable tasks on Genesis. The engine in which
every material class shares one scene and a coding agent is the intended user: Genesis (Dec 2024, "combines multiple solvers for
robotics in one platform") and Alloy, 2026-11-12. The enabling piece and the suite arrived in the same week; a solo port of six
incumbents' tasks starting now would land behind both, into a lane that produced fourteen non-rigid suites or data engines in twelve
months. The claim's own why_now field says "None specific", and the search confirms that the pieces it was waiting for were already
other people's releases.

## 5. Killing experiment check

**P-W43-J06-a** (the $0, two-day overlap table): answered by the fetched texts without running. The incumbents number at least
fifteen (section 2), RoboVerse already migrates one of them and states the mechanism, ManiLadder holds 40+ deformable tasks across
1D/2D/3D with standardised protocols, and no incumbent aggregator reports adoption as a column (RoboVerse's Table IV runs two
"simple tasks" for VLAs "constrained with time and resources"). The pre-registered kill — over 70% re-packaging and no adoption
growth — fires on the record; cost $0.

**C-W44-16** (GAUGE's trajectory error on Gym-Anything's agent-written cloth, tissue and rigid stacks; ~10 GPU-h, $50, 12 days): under
one GPU-week, so Gate 1 holds, but its claim attachment was wrong on the day it was filed — the record itself says its surprise
"belongs to the fidelity question (J03/J04), not to adoption". With J06 killed it is an orphan unless re-attached. Design hints for the
re-attachment: add Alloy as a fourth engine row (its cloth is Newton VBD, its liquid its own SPH; its Table 4 closed-form checks and
its limitations page give the pre-registered expectation — the drape of a woven card "differently from the same fabric on a drape
tester, even when converged" is a stated textile error before any GAUGE family is run); separate "agent-written solver"
(Gym-Anything's backward-Euler cloth) from "agent-configured engine" (Alloy, Newton underneath), which is the contrast the W44 design
did not name; and keep the 10% Newton port validation. Its own concept-level kill search is owed under its new parent before any
design red team.

**The residue probe** (cross-engine ranking stability on a shipped non-rigid suite): take ManiLadder's deformable suites (Genesis PBD
cloth, MPM clay, SPH liquid), rebuild a sample of scenes in Alloy (Newton VBD cloth, MPM, SPH) from the released task
configurations, retrain the fixed-data flow-matching baseline in both, and read whether the per-task success rates and the
difficulty levels agree. Write $s^{e}_{k}$ for the success rate of the calibration policy on task $k$ in engine $e$; the reading is the
rank correlation $\rho(s^{\mathrm{Genesis}}, s^{\mathrm{Alloy}})$ over the deformable tasks and the count of tasks whose level changes.
Cost: the ManiLadder protocol is 20 demonstrations and one small policy per task; ten tasks in two engines is on the order of 20
GPU-hours plus a week of scene rebuilding. Kill line for a J03-style headline: $\rho \ge 0.9$ and no level change means the
non-rigid ladder is engine-independent and the ruler has nothing to add on this suite. This is a probe for CL-W43-J03 (does fidelity
predict what policies do) and a scene set for CL-W43-J04; it is not a claim about adoption and not a suite.

## 7. Lessons for prompts/checklist.md

- [ ] 2026-11-15 — An engine's own "what does not work yet" page is the limitation section when the paper's scope section delegates to it: Alloy sec. 5 is two sentences and a URL, and the page holds the measured textile, liquid and soft-solid departures from reality that decide the material-behaviour clause of CL-W43-J06. Fetch the owner's limitation page before quoting the paper's scope paragraph. (CL-W43-J06.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — A unification paper's abstract uses the plain phrase, not the community's term: Alloy says "share one scene" and matched none of "multi-material" / "unified simulator" / "two-way coupling" (Q2: 0 on target); the concurrent-twin form (the materials named, no role words) found it at once. For engine releases, run the material list as the query before the unification vocabulary. (CL-W43-J06.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — A capture whose record already says "alive only as the scene set for another claim" and whose probe's record says its surprise "belongs to" another claim is misfiled on the day it is captured; the kill search's work is then re-attachment, not discovery. At `/capture` and `/claims`, when a probe's surprise names a different claim, attach it there and leave the suite claim as a thought. (CL-W43-J06, C-W44-16.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — "Nobody has an organised non-rigid suite" expired inside the three weeks between the claim's last re-ranking (2026-11-08) and this search (ManiLadder 2026-11-12, Alloy 2026-11-12, ClothDojo 2026-10-31); a claim whose kill search is owed for more than two weeks in a one-paper-a-week lane is re-ranked below every claim that has had one, so the automatic budget reaches it before the lane does. (CL-W43-J06; rule 6's "a claim that waits three weeks for its kill search is a race lost", observed.)
