## Checklist lessons (appended to prompts/checklist.md)

- 2026-10-25 — A quotation in why_now, target_value or a limitation_quote must be grep-verified in the paper's text before it enters a record; a card's information_asymmetry inference written as a quote fabricated the requester in print for C-W43-23 (2610.26836 has zero occurrences of 'oracle' or 'compaction').
- 2026-10-25 — A judge-free oracle built on a benchmark's reference solutions names the split that ships them; AppWorld withholds solutions on test_normal and test_challenge, so a probe costed on 168 test-normal tasks had no oracle on the corpus it named.
- 2026-10-25 — The first step of any value-matching probe is to run the matcher over the reference solutions and report coverage (composed, task-given, excluded, auth shares) before the probe is costed; here 25.7% of needed values are composed or task-given and 19 of 35 published failures sit in scenarios whose failing value is composed, which fires the protocol kill before any run.
- 2026-10-25 — A census of failures after a boundary needs the uncompressed arm as its control before 'fails by' is written; a presence label (value in or out of the summary) is not a cause, and TRACE's re-audit retained 0 of 3 presence-based labels; SJC Table 3 bounds compaction-attributable failures at 31.5% of post-compaction failures.
- 2026-10-25 — When two attackers derive opposite numbers from the same protocol (8.6% and 51% unsourced on SJC Table 11), the protocol's normalization rule is the unwritten part; the matcher (typed fields, atomic decomposition, summary membership) is pre-registered before any quantifier in the claim sentence.
- 2026-10-25 — The bootstrap cluster on AppWorld is the scenario (one template, three variants), not the task; a kill clause with a lower-bound condition is power-checked at the scenario level (half-width about 0.3 at n = 35, about 0.25 at n = 90 under full correlation), and 'informative either way' is written only when the band between the claim's quantifier and the kill's threshold is empty.
- 2026-10-25 — On stateful-shell harnesses (AppWorld through ACON/SJC) the produced corpus is defined at the API-response layer, not printed stdout; AppWorld's RequestTracker logs resolved call arguments, responses need a one-line wrapper, and a variable that survives a boundary is a channel the LLM context does not show.
- 2026-10-25 — A lab-position verdict that says 'a day's audit on owned logs' is checked against what the oracle needs; production logs carry no reference solution, so only reference-free labels (emitted value in any tool output; in the summary) transfer.
- 2026-10-25 — An unsourced-persistence estimator must test membership in the summary ($w \in M$) and exclude the never-fetched cell, or it is near-entailed by the build share; 'came from no environment output' alone is not persistence.
- 2026-10-25 — A claim sentence carries one quantified statement and its kill fires at that number; three statements with one kill at a different number left a falsifying band that fired nothing, and a headline noun ('not by length') that no cell touches is not tested by the probe that claims it.
