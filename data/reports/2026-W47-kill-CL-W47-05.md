Glossary (each term once). **Lead / worker** — the agent that assigns documents and writes the report, and the agents that read assigned
documents and send findings up. **Required** — the seed paper's policy in which workers read partitioned documents and the lead reads
none. **None** — one agent reads and writes. **Planted pair $G_i = \{A_i, B_i\}$** — the two conflicting values of one quantity the
benchmark wrote into different documents of task $i$. **Same / Split** — one worker read both values, or only the union of workers did.
**Pair completeness** $PC$ — the record-linkage term for the share of true pairs that a blocking step puts in one block; here the share of
read pairs that one worker holds, $PC = \frac{1}{N}\sum_i z_i$ with $z_i = 1$ when some worker read both $A_i$ and $B_i$ and $N$ the
number of pairs anyone read. **Permutation null** $\mathbb{E}_{\pi'}[PC]$ — $PC$ averaged over uniform random re-assignments $\pi'$ of the
same documents to the same number of workers. **Hidden profile** — the group-decision paradigm (Stasser and Titus 1985) in which a group
fails to pool a fact only one member holds. **Flag rate** $s_X$ — the probability that a report written under arm $X$ names the planted
conflict, given that the input the report was written from carried both values. **Cross-section defect** — 2605.26174's term for a
contradiction between two distant sections of one document, visible only when both are in view. **Cliff Depth Ratio** — 2605.26174's
single-agent detection minus orchestrated detection, divided by single-agent detection.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed as a claim (Gate 2B: the first clause's surprise is the stated, pre-registered and confirmed thesis of the nearest paper and
the seed paper's own design recommendation; Gate 4: the if_true action is the seed's own fix; Gate 1 for the cheapest probe: its $0 arm
dies at step 0 because the seed released neither traces, profiler nor planted edits). A probe-sized residue survives for the second
clause, redesign-owed, not a claim.** The claim's first clause says planted conflicts vanish because of how the lead splits the documents,
not because workers summarise. Fukui, *A Universal Cliff and a Design Fingerprint: Cross-Section Defect Detection Under LLM Orchestration*
(2605.26174, 2026-05-25), partitioned four long documents with sixteen embedded cross-section contradictions across five workers and ten
models, found detection falling by two-thirds or more in every model that could find the defects alone, and wrote the clause as its
mechanism — "The cliff is mechanism-derived — it follows from dividing a document among agents who each hold only a part — and is not
closed by scale or by extended reasoning"; "The partition is a property of the architecture, not a role any agent occupies" — then
pre-registered and ran a confirmatory re-run under corrected assignments and concluded "Per the pre-registered rule, the cliff is a property
of partitioning structure, not of assignment-label content." It also wrote the claim's if_true consequence: "offering the user a complete
delegation trace does not repair this—the trace shows who did what, not the relation that no one was assigned to hold." The seed paper
2611.22245 itself attributes the handoff loss to the partition ("workers summarizing isolated partitions lack the cross-document context",
sec. 5) and ends with the claim's recipe ("these findings caution against rigid, non-overlapping context partitioning", sec. 5). What is
unoccupied is the permutation null on a lead's realised partition; but its outcome is a harness fact (how many workers the lead spawned and
whether it assigned whole directories), not a belief anyone has stated, and a $0 re-read of the seed's Table 1 shows the null has no value
without the worker count, which the paper does not print: the observed Split shares imply $2.4$, $2.4$ and $3.8$ equiprobable workers per
model against a policy that "permit[s] up to ten concurrent workers". The second clause — a report written through a summary flags more of
the conflicts it keeps than a report written from the raw context — is unoccupied as a measurement on long raw contexts, but the nearest
controlled comparison points the other way on short contexts (2610.37624's written evidence summary at or below the all-facts memory: 72.0
versus 73.1 on Qwen, 58.7 versus 72.6 on Llama), and its $0 arm also dies at step 0 (the None contexts are unreleased proxy captures). It
survives as a footnote-sized probe on the public Harvey LAB tasks with the design requirements in sec. 5.

## 5. Killing-experiment check, the $0 re-read, and what a residue would need

**C-W47-10 (partition null).** Step 0 is dead: no traces. The step-2 arithmetic the probe pre-registered runs at $0 on Table 1 and is
reported here. For a pair whose two values sit in two different documents, a uniform assignment of documents to $w$ equiprobable workers
co-locates the pair with probability $1/w$, so the Split share among read pairs is
$$ q_{\mathrm{split}} = 1 - \frac{1}{w}, \qquad w_{\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{1}{1 - q_{\mathrm{split}}}, $$
where $q_{\mathrm{split}}$ is the Split count divided by the Source Reads total in the card's Table 1 and $w_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is the number of
equiprobable workers that would produce it by chance. The seed's cells give $q_{\mathrm{split}} = 0.582$, $0.578$, $0.739$ and $0.632$
(GPT-5.6, GPT-6, Haiku, pooled Required), hence $w_{\mathrm{eff}} = 2.40$, $2.37$, $3.83$, $2.72$; under Optional, Haiku's $q_{\mathrm{split}} =
0.573$ ($w_{\mathrm{eff}} = 2.34$) and the pooled $0.189$ ($1.23$, because the GPT leads rarely delegate). Against a lead that actually spawned
five equiprobable workers the random null is $q_{\mathrm{split}} = 0.800$ and the observed shares sit $0.06$ to $0.22$ below it; against ten
workers, $0.900$ and $0.16$ to $0.32$ below. So the claim's expected outcome ("within 10 points of the null") holds only if the realised
worker counts are about two to four, or if the leads assign by directory and the planted directories are few; either way the result is a
description of the harness's delegation behaviour, not a belief about delegation. The probe cannot be graded without the worker count,
which the authors have and the paper omits. **Verdict: killed with the claim; the arithmetic above is the footnote, and it is one
sentence.**

**C-W47-11 (summary salience at fixed context).** Step 0 is dead: the None captures are unreleased. The fallback is a run on public LAB
due-diligence tasks with own planted numerical pairs (the seed's recipe: replace selected appearances of a recurring value in designated
directories), one agent per task, then three report arms from the identical captured context — raw (R), through a return-style findings
summary (S), summary prepended to raw (SR) — with $s_R$, $s_S$, $s_{SR}$, the keep rate $\kappa_S$ (the probability that the summary carries
both values given the context did) and the token distance $d_i$ between the two values. About $150 of API spend or 50 GPU-hours with an
open-weight model, five days, under one GPU-week. What survival requires of the design, for the design red team if it ever runs:

1. **Pre-register against the two fetched data points that go the other way** (CrossProfile B3 at or below all-facts on both models;
   HiddenBench's passive summarisation at 0.241 and 0.713). The residue's mechanism — a summary collapses the token distance between the two
   values — predicts a gain only when $d_i$ is large; the pre-registration states the distance band in which $s_S - s_R \ge 0.15$ is
