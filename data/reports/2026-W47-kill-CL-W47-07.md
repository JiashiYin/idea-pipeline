**Glossary (each term once).** *Success checker* $c$: the benchmark's hand-written map from the simulator state $x_t$ to one bit.
*First-hit readout*: the episode is recorded as a success at the first step $t^\ast$ with $c(x_{t^\ast}) = 1$ and ends there.
*Persistence window* $\tau$: the verdict $C_\tau = \mathbf{1}[\exists\, t : c(x_s) = 1\ \forall s \in [t, t + \tau]]$; the shipped
readout is $\tau = 0$. *Flip rate* $\phi_j(\tau)$: the share of paired seeded episodes whose verdict differs between the shipped
setting and setting $j$. *Repeat floor* $\phi_0(\tau)$: the same share between two runs at the shipped setting. *Excess flip rate*
$\psi_j(\tau) = \phi_j(\tau) - \phi_0(\tau)$. *Same-seed placebo*: a second run of the base policy on the same seed, the null for a
paired count. *Common random numbers (CRN)*: the policy's sampling noise drawn identically in both arms. *Contact offset* $d_c$: the
distance at which PhysX begins generating contact points before surfaces touch. *Physics step* $h$: the integration interval, 4 ms
on both benchmarks. *Fixed-delay arm*: the scene advanced by a chosen number of physics steps before each chunk executes.

## 5. Killing-experiment check and the $0 checks

**$0 check 1 — the floor from released numbers (P4 of the import; the expected result of C-W47-14).** From 2610.06921 Table 1, with
$S$ the base success rate, $r_0$ the same-seed placebo rescue rate (share of failed base episodes that succeed on a plain re-run) and
$b_0$ the placebo breakage rate (share of base successes that fail on a plain re-run), the same-seed pairwise flip rate is

$$\phi_0 = (1 - S)\, r_0 + S\, b_0 ,$$

and a base re-run has zero net effect by construction, so the two terms are equal. bbh: $S = 0.540$, $r_0 = 0.432$, $b_0 = 0.368$,
$\phi_0 = 39.7\%$; qr: $0.608 / 0.433 / 0.279$, $33.9\%$; a2b: $0.383 / 0.208 / 0.336$, $25.7\%$; a2br: $0.340 / 0.215 / 0.418$,
$28.4\%$. Unseeded $\pi_{0.5}$ on RoboTwin 2.0 flips 26–40% of episodes against itself, and 0% once seeded per episode (their §3.2).
The import pre-registered $\phi_0(0)$ between 1 and 4 pp: the premise (a deterministic simulator) is right, the magnitude is an order
of magnitude off in one reading and exactly zero in the other. For RoboDyna, with overall $S = 0.19$ and a homogeneous per-seed
success probability, $2 S (1 - S) = 30.8\%$ against the reported $85 / 700 = 12.1\%$; the true per-seed probabilities spread toward 0
and 1 lower the floor, and the paper gives nothing to compute it. C-W47-14's "expected" outcome ($F(0,0') \geq 0.5\, F(0,27)$) and
its "uninformative" outcome ($F(0,0') = 0$) are the two readings of one flag; its "kill" outcome (low floor, rising dose curve) is
reachable only after per-episode re-seeding, which is 2611.02101's design.

**$0 check 2 — step 0 of C-W47-14 from the release.** `_base_task.py`: `n = int(round(seconds / dt))` with `dt = 1/250`, so delays
are whole physics steps and $d = 1$ exists; `advance_world` runs "while the robot holds its last command"; success is checked during
the advance ("a task solved during inference (the ball lands in the already-closed gripper) counts"); `configure_async_inference(
enabled, cap_s=1.0, fixed_s=None, skip_first=True)` ships the fixed delay, a one-second cap per charge and an exemption for the
