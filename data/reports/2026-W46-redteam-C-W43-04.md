Glossary (full definitions in the record): **role switch** — flipping a running instance between the prefill and decode roles;
**$n_P$, $n_D$** — prefill and decode instance counts in a pool of $G = 8$; **$B_P$** — uncached prompt tokens queued or in flight;
**$K_D$** — KV tokens decoding or waiting; **Prorsa's sizing rule** — $n_P = \lceil W_P (1+\delta)/(S\,\theta_P) \rceil$, $n_D =
\lceil (K_D + \hat K_D)(1+\delta)/C_{KV} \rceil$, $W_P = B_P + W_{\mathrm{fut}}$, $S = 10$ s, $\theta_P$ prefill tokens per second per
instance, $C_{KV}$ KV capacity per decode instance, $\delta$ a safety margin; **Prorsa-zeroed** — the same with $W_{\mathrm{fut}} =
\hat K_D = 0$; **damping** — a minimum dwell and a cool-down; **headroom** $H = T_{\mathrm{static}}/\hat T_{\mathrm{oracle}} - 1$
($\hat T_{\mathrm{oracle}}$ from an achievable schedule search, bracketed by a fluid relaxation $H_{\mathrm{fluid}}$); **capture
ratio** $\rho_{\mathrm{arm}} = (T_{\mathrm{static}} - T_{\mathrm{arm}})/(T_{\mathrm{static}} - \hat T_{\mathrm{oracle}})$, negative
when an arm loses to static.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Red-teamed (survives, narrowed).** 19 objections, 5 killing and 14 limiting; 13 answered, 6 conceded with rewrites, 0 open. No
killing objection stands: the fit-to-target calibration gate was removed (constants are fixed from a published profile before any arm
runs and Prorsa's 1.0–1.44x band becomes a reported consistency row); the v1 "dynamic program" oracle was never a dynamic program and is
replaced by an achievable-schedule search bracketed by a fluid bound, whose bias is shown to be conservative for both the kill and the
confirmation of the claim; the toy's "perfect forecast" held a strict subset of Prorsa's information and is replaced by a clone-ahead
forecast that holds a superset; the stationary closed loop alone would have measured a world without Prorsa's lever, so a
non-stationary drift cell at Prorsa's own 1.74x ratio swing becomes the primary cell. The toy replay already says what the probe is
sensitive to: an undamped controller that re-sizes every slot loses to the static split even with perfect foresight ($\rho$ from
$-0.05$ to $-45$), while damped observation rules capture 0.3–0.93 of a 5–13% headroom at $N = 1024$ — so the probe isolates the
forecast increment inside Prorsa's rule at matched damping (clause 08a), keeps the literal threshold-rule contrast as 08b, and
pre-registers the regime-dependent sign of headroom against concurrency as 08c. Cost: 0 GPU-hours, $0, 7 days (about 200 CPU-hours);
an optional 16 GPU-hour, about $60 arm calibrates the shipped SGLang flip. Claim grade: **proper (narrow)** — the headline narrows to
the forecast increment, one consumer community, race window about 1–2x time-to-result, timing inputs partly synthetic.

## 1. The design under attack

**Statement (v2, condensed).** A length-driven discrete-time replay of agentic rollout on $G = 8$ instances (per-instance decode KV pools,
chunked prefill at 4096, prefix-cache hit ratio swept), fed by public per-turn lengths (80k SWE-agent trajectories; math lengths from a
public rollout release) with tool timing from SWE-agent `.traj` `execution_time` if its listing is confirmed, else a labelled lognormal
sweep; a synthetic general-agent workload labelled as such. Cells: synchronous steps of $B \in \{256, 1024, 4096\}$; asynchronous closed
loops at over-dispatch $f \in \{1, 2, 4\}$ of $B = 256$, stationary and non-stationary (training-step segments whose length mix moves the
prefill-to-decode ratio by Prorsa's reported 1.74x); three switch-cost models — Prorsa's 5-s cancel-and-resend, 30 s, and SGLang's
shipped drain-to-idle flip with prefix-cache release (PR 28403); five trajectory-set draws per cell. Arms on one trace: best static
split; a two-by-two inside Prorsa's sizing rule, forecast {clone-ahead perfect, perturbed to WAPE 5.4–14.4%, zeroed} × damping {none —
as the paper writes it, FluidRole persistence and cool-down swept}; the FluidRole hysteresis rule on $(\mathrm{PPI}, \mathrm{DPI})$ with
thresholds swept; the achievable oracle and the fluid bound. Readouts: $T$ (makespan and $T_{95}$ in sync cells), $[\hat H,
H_{\mathrm{fluid}}]$, $\rho$ per arm with a paired $t$-interval over draws, switch counts, re-prefilled tokens, regime and provenance labels.

**Claims tested.**
- **08a (the narrowed headline, filed as claim_headline_v2):** at $N \ge 1024$ asynchronous, Prorsa's rule with its ten-second forecast
  zeroed captures, at matched damping, within ten points of what the same rule captures with the forecast: $\rho_{\mathrm{fc}} -
  \rho_{\mathrm{zero}} \le 0.10$ wherever $\hat H \ge 0.05$. **Kill:** $\rho_{\mathrm{fc}} - \rho_{\mathrm{zero}} \ge 0.30$ with the band
  excluding 0.10, under the 5-s model, in two of three workloads with $\hat H \ge 0.05$; primary cell the non-stationary drift loop.
- **08b (the literal headline):** the FluidRole-style hysteresis rule captures within ten points of the damped perfect-forecast arm.
  **Kill:** difference $\ge 0.30$, band excluding 0.10, same conditions. The kill report's original clause ($\hat H \ge 0.15$,
  forecaster $\ge 0.8$, hysteresis $\le 0.5$) is reported as the special case.
- **08c (mechanism, no kill):** headroom's sign against concurrency is regime-dependent — falls with $N$ compute-bound, does not
  KV-bound; a rise in the KV-bound regime confirms Prorsa's capacity-drift lever and rewrites the claim's gate-4 sentence, not the claim.
- **Guard:** $\hat H < 0.05$ in every $N \ge 1024$ asynchronous cell makes 08a and 08b "not resolvable" and is itself the pre-registered
  result (role switching a dead lever at steady state at this concurrency). **Secondary line:** the same guard under the drain model
  answers the SGLang roadmap's router-policy item for the shipped mechanism.

## 7. Lessons for `prompts/checklist.md`

- [ ] 2026-11-16 — A "dynamic program over (slot, control)" is not an oracle unless the system state is a function of (slot, control); for a queueing replay it is a function of the whole schedule, so the hindsight optimum is a schedule search that returns an achievable bound — write which side of the true optimum the bound sits on and which direction that pushes each kill clause before pre-registering a capture ratio. (C-W43-04.)
- [ ] 2026-11-16 — A "perfect forecast" arm in a replay must be checked against the occupant's predictor's input list: the toy's forecast saw only tool returns while Prorsa's sees injections, sandbox start-ups and decode completions; an information arm that holds a subset of the occupant's information makes the value-of-information clause small by construction. (C-W43-04.)
- [ ] 2026-11-16 — A calibration gate that re-fits constants until the occupant's headline number appears is a fit, not a calibration; fix constants from a published profile first and report the occupant's band as a consistency row. (C-W43-04.)
- [ ] 2026-11-16 — Run the undamped form of a controller before comparing information sets: an undamped rule re-sized every slot lost to the static split with perfect foresight in every toy cell, so a forecast-versus-observation contrast without matched damping measures churn. (C-W43-04.)
