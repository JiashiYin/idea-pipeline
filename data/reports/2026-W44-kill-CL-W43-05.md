Glossary (each term once): **compaction** — replacing an agent's earlier context with a model-written summary when the
window fills. **Unsourced entry** — a summary line no environment output or earlier summary supports (SJC's word is
"unsupported"). **Omission / commission** — the summary dropped a needed fact / the summary asserts a wrong or
unsupported one. **Uncompressed control** — the same task continued from the full history (SJC's "No Compression";
Compact-Bench's PRE arm). **Census** — a count of failures by cause. **Closure cell** — the needed value was in view and the
agent acted on something else (from the C-W43-23 record).

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed as written; a residue claim survives with the opposite noun.** The concept-level question was: has anyone
measured, with an uncompressed control, that failures after compaction are fabricated or unsupported entries rather than
dropped ones, and at what share? Nobody has run that census with the control. But the headline's first clause is Source-Judged
Compaction's own abstract — "83% of failed runs trace back to an action that carries out a specific summary entry, most of
which no environment output supports" (2610.20927, 2026-10-14) — and its `if_true` ("compactors label or delete unsourced
entries by default") is SJC's shipped recipe with a +8.9 Pass3 result, so the claim is a restatement of a published paper
(Gate 6: occupied; Gate 2B: the pre-registered surprise equals the field's stated knowledge as this lane wrote it down).
Every controlled measurement in print then points away from the noun: Compact-Bench's paired PRE/POST diagnosis finds the
repair evidence retained in 4 of 4 stable damages ("all five audited tokens survive, yet the continuation fails") and its
one commission detector (a completed write described as pending) "flags zero boundaries" of 224 production compactions;
transparent-compact's probes with control turns find that in all 7 confident-wrong answers "the correct value was still in
the summary" and in 5 "a competing value from a later look-alike episode stood beside it"; Governance Decay's constraint
benchmark finds "when the constraint survives the summary, violation remains 0%, but when it is dropped, violation reaches
38%"; 2610.31778 and CONPACT measure dropped constraints with before/after controls. SJC itself concedes "the
unsupported entry raises the chance of the error rather than determining it" and that its census "relies on annotations
produced by LLM annotators" with no control arm annotated. The second clause, "not by length", is tested by no paper and by
none of the claim's probes except C-W43-08's matched-count and section-drop arms. What survives is a different sentence:
**the compaction-attributable excess of failures (differenced against the uncompressed arm) is sourced values that lost
their binding — stale or look-alike entries — and dropped constraints, not entries nothing supports; and the provenance
judge's gain is a section-and-length effect.** That residue is a new claim with C-W44-11 (the census with its full-history
pair) and C-W43-08 (the only length control) as its probes; proper-shaped, not golden (race window about 1x
time-to-result).

## 3. Pre-window precedents (named with their tricks)

- **2005.00661 Maynez et al. (2020)** — human-annotate summaries for intrinsic versus extrinsic hallucination (misused source content versus content with no source); the summarization field's standard commission split, "highly prone to hallucinate content that is unfaithful to the input document". SJC's "unsupported" is extrinsic hallucination under a new name.
- **2406.03487 (2024-06)** — benchmark LLM dialogue summaries by span-level human labels and coin "Circumstantial Inference": "LLMs often generate plausible inferences, supported by circumstantial evidence in the conversation, that lack direct evidence". SJC's unsourced plan bullets (Open TODOs 67.7% unsourced) are circumstantial inferences in an agent log; the category was named two years ago.
- **2104.13346 FRANK (2021)** — a typology of factual errors in summaries with per-category human proportions; the pre-window form of "label each summary error by type".
- **2402.13249 TofuEval (2024)** — sentence-level factual-consistency labels on topic-focused dialogue summaries with an error taxonomy; LLM judges perform poorly on them.
- **2510.00615 ACON (2025-10-01)** — rewrite compression guidelines from LLM analyses of the agent's failures; the LLM-read failure taxonomy the lane inherited (three weeks before the window).
- **2508.21433 Complexity Trap (2025-08)** — drop old observations instead of summarizing them at matched solve rate; the "omission costs nothing" belief the controlled papers now test.

## 4. Verdicts by component of the headline

**Clause 1, "compaction summaries fail by unsourced persistence".** Occupied by SJC 2610.20927 as a census (71% of 35
analyzed failures follow an unsupported or contradicted entry, LLM-annotated) and as a recipe (delete unsourced entries:
+8.9 Pass3 AppWorld GPT). What SJC does not have is the control: its uncompressed reference is in Table 3 but its failures
were never traced, so the 71% is a presence share conditional on failure, and SJC's own Table 3 bounds compaction-attributable
failures at 31.5% of the Prompting arm's (C-W43-23 red team). The controlled papers that exist measure the other nouns:
dropped constraints (Governance Decay, CONPACT, 2610.31778, Compaction Cliff 2608.22752: "53% of safety rules after one
compaction round and 10% after five"), sourced-but-unbound values (transparent-compact 7 of 7; Reclaim; 2610.36308), lost
progress with the ingredients retained (Compact-Bench 4 of 4). A fabricated-entry cell with a control has a count of zero
in print (Compact-Bench's completed-write-as-pending detector: 0 of 224). **Gate 2B fails**: the claim's expected result is
SJC's stated result, and the only pre-registered surprise left is the one C-W44-11 already carries, that invented-entry
failures are under one in ten, which is the claim's negation.

**Clause 2, "not by length".** Tested by no neighbour and by none of the claim's probes except C-W43-08 (matched-count random
deletion and section-drop against SJC's judge). 2610.27301 supplies the random-deletion control on other corpora and
finds that question-conditioned selection does no better than random on later questions; 2610.36308 shows one sourced
competitor beats three unrelated statements at matched size (length is not the driver of stale selection). Neither touches
compaction summaries in an agent loop. The clause is untested, not refuted; it belongs to the residue.

**Consequence, "compactors label or delete unsourced entries by default; evaluations add a provenance axis".** The delete
half is shipped (SJC). The label half has a published reader-side negative for small readers (2610.36308: statement-number
provenance not consumed at 7B-14B). The provenance axis exists in evaluation form for memory stores (HaluMem's fabrication
cell; TRUSTMEM's faithfulness) and in security form (Compaction Laundering). No evaluation adds it for compaction summaries
of agent trajectories; that gap is a service unless the residue's census shows the cell is non-empty.

## 5. The two closest papers' limitation text (verbatim, grep-verified)

**SJC 2610.20927, Limitations:** "SJC filters by provenance, not correctness, and the two are largely independent:
supported and unsupported entries are correct at similar rates (92.2% against 90.6%; Appendix G)." ... "In multi-turn
settings, earlier user messages are themselves a source and would need to be treated as one; we have not evaluated that
case." ... "Finally, the failure analysis in Section 3 relies on annotations produced by LLM annotators under a written
protocol." Section 5.5: "The unsupported entry raises the chance of the error rather than determining it: the third
Prompting run carries the same entry and still succeeds." Appendix D: "All 44 failed runs of the Prompting baseline on the
training split were traced by LLM annotators following a fixed written protocol." Section 5.1: "We use No Compression,
which retains the full interaction history, as the uncompressed reference." (The reference arm's failures are not traced
anywhere in the paper.)

**Compact-Bench 2610.31710, Limitations:** "Results are conditional on the tested repository repairs, continuation agent,
and budget. Detail-retention scores capture literal recurrence, while later production-boundary estimates use small,
changing session subsets." Section 4.4: "Pytest-7571 is particularly informative: all five audited tokens survive, yet
the continuation fails." ... "The cases expose a distinction between preserving repair ingredients and preserving the
progress that connects them to a completed edit." Appendix C: "The completed-write detector flags zero boundaries under
the matching rules in Appendix L."

**Third, transparent-compact 2610.20116, Section 5.1:** "Omission is not the only failure. In each of the 7 cases in which
a detail was first answered correctly and later answered confidently wrong, the correct value was still in the summary.
In 5 of them a competing value from a later look-alike episode stood beside it, and the context that had separated the
two was gone." ... "One summary also silently rewrote the day of a release freeze, an error the probes did not score, so
they understate summary errors." Limitations: "control turns for the commitment probes were run on Qwen only".

## 6. Why not done (the controlled commission/omission census on natural failures)

Partly done by hand four times (SJC, 35 runs, no control; TRACE annotation, 14 trajectories; Compact-Bench, 4 damages;
transparent-compact, 7 confident-wrong answers), never at a share with a control. It is hard because the needed-value
oracle requires a reference solution (AppWorld train+dev only; production logs carry none — C-W43-23 red team) and a
naturalistic corpus pulls the field back to a judge (2606.25449's sentence). The enabling pieces: Compact-Bench's paired
PRE/POST continuations with code and data (2026-10-22); HSC-Bench's full-history arm for six receivers with typed
criteria (2026-10-27); SJC's public harness with the No Compression arm (2026-10-14); the typed matcher and
scenario-cluster protocol written by the C-W43-23 red team (2026-10-25). All landed within six weeks. The group that holds
the SJC logs is the natural first mover and has not been asked; that email is the residue's step 0.

## 8. Killing-experiment check (for the residue)

Restated: on a corpus with a full-history pair (Compact-Bench's 35 repairs x 14 summarizers, public; HSC-Bench's 739 x 6
on request; the SJC harness as fallback), take every criterion or test failed under the summary and passed under full
history by the same agent, and label it by the typed matcher: dropped (needed value in the history, not in the summary),
corrupted (a different sourced value in the field), competing-sourced (the needed value and a sourced look-alike both in
view — transparent-compact's 5 of 7), in-view-unused (Compact-Bench's 4 of 4), followed-unsourced (the reported value
equals a summary entry that matches nothing in the history). Report each cell's share $\pi_c$ with a scenario-family
bootstrap and a placebo message's chance-match rate; the headline quantifier is one number, the followed-unsourced share.
Cost: $0 to $300, 0 GPU-hours, about 3 weeks; under one GPU-week. Kill of the residue (pre-registered): the followed-unsourced
cell's clustered 95% lower bound above 0.10 and above the placebo rate for three of six receivers — then CL-W43-05's noun
was right and the residue is wrong (C-W44-11's `kills_if`, unchanged). Second arm (C-W43-08): if SJC's judge beats both
the matched-count random delete and the section drop by 5 Pass3 points, provenance carries information beyond length and
section and the residue's second half is dead. Both outcomes are citable.

## 9. Verdict

**killed** (claim as written). Reason: SJC 2610.20927 occupies the headline's first clause and its consequence (census and
delete recipe in print), so the surprise equals the field's stated knowledge (Gate 2B, Gate 6); the second clause is

constraints, lost progress and sourced-but-unbound values, not in entries nothing supports. Weak survival: not
applicable. Re-open: not applicable; the residue is a new claim.

**Narrowest surviving headline (new claim, to be captured):** "Against the full-history pair, compaction-attributable
failures are sourced values that lost their binding (stale or look-alike entries) and dropped constraints, not entries
nothing supports — under one in ten — and the provenance judge's +8.9 Pass3 is matched by section drop or matched-count

**Probe recommendation.** C-W44-11 is the cheapest probe of the residue and needs one addition: a competing-sourced cell

## 10. Checklist lessons (2026-11-01)

- A claim seeded from a paper's own abstract carries that paper's headline as its `current_belief` and cannot pre-register a
  surprise against it (CL-W43-05's first clause was SJC's abstract sentence; the `if_true` was SJC's recipe). At `/claims`
  time, grep the seed paper's abstract against the headline before grading.
- A commission ruler can be in print as a zero: Compact-Bench's completed-write-as-pending detector flags 0 of 224
  production boundaries (Appendix C). Search the appendices of the paired-replay papers for the cell's detector before
  calling the cell unmeasured.
- The summarization field named the lane's "unsourced plan bullet" in 2024 ("Circumstantial Inference", 2406.03487) and in
  2020 (extrinsic hallucination, 2005.00661); a provenance axis for agent summaries is an import of those taxonomies and must
  say so.
