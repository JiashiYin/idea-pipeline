# Kill search — CL-W43-11: an agent served under KV-cache eviction is not the agent its transcript describes

Date: 2026-11-01 (W44 weekly run). Level: claim (concept), per CLAUDE.md rule 17. Claim `CL-W43-11` (L3; L2, L4): "An agent
served under KV-cache eviction is not the agent its transcript describes: it keeps using tool outputs it can no longer see,
and a trainer recomputing log-probabilities from the text does not reproduce the policy it sampled from." Probes: C-W43-24
(ghost information: KV drop vs the same drop as text), C-W43-25 (served-vs-text divergence by turn offset since the last drop),
C-W44-09 (growth law of the prefix log-ratio: diffusion vs drift), C-W44-10 (does the eviction term reach the gradient).
Procedure: `prompts/03_kill.md`; 22 in-window arXiv API queries and 5 all-time queries (every string in §2), 56 abstracts
fetched by `id_list`, three full texts read (MemoryWalker 2609.00865, Memento 2604.09852, Context Language Models
2609.37725; saved under the session scratchpad `kills/CL-W43-11/html/`). Semantic Scholar skipped (`degraded: s2`). Every
neighbour below was fetched this session; depth marked [abstract] or [full]. Every quotation was grep-verified in the fetched
text.

Glossary (each term once). *KV cache*: the per-token key and value vectors a transformer stores so it does not recompute
attention over the prefix. *Eviction*: deleting some of those entries. *Ghost information*: what a retained entry still
encodes about tokens that were visible when it was computed and are now evicted. *Text recompute*: a trainer re-running the
model on the transcript to get the log-probabilities it needs for the policy gradient. *Conditioning*: the exact set of
entries each token's query attended to when it was generated. *Restart ablation*: regenerate the same text but rebuild the
cache from the visible text alone, so the only difference is the cache state. *4D mask*: a per-query attention mask
(batch, heads, query, key) that lets each token in one training forward attend to exactly the entries it saw at rollout.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed — both halves of the headline are published positives, in the neighbours' own words, with the fix shipped.**
The serving half ("keeps using tool outputs it can no longer see") is Memento 2604.09852 (April 2026) §6.2.1, which ran
exactly C-W43-24's one-bit test — same generated text, cache retained as computed while the evicted block was visible versus
cache rebuilt from the visible text — and found a 15.3-point drop on AIME24 when the ghost channel is removed (66.1% to
50.8%, Qwen3-8B); it is cited as the reason Suffix Cache Reuse works in Context Language Models 2609.37725 (September 2026),
whose appendix states the mechanism in one sentence ("Because the reused states were computed under the pre-edit context,
Suffix Cache Reuse approximates re-prefilling"). The trainer half ("a trainer recomputing from the text does not reproduce the
policy it sampled from") is the title of MemoryWalker 2609.00865 (September 2026): "Stop Training Agents on Contexts They
Never Saw", which measures the train–rollout log-probability gap at 0.366 against a no-compression floor of 0.014 (26x) on
eviction-heavy batches, proves that a packed 4D attention mask built from the harness's eviction records is gradient-exact
(its Theorem 1), and ships it on the Slime stack; Memento App. A.2.3 already trains under physical KV compaction in a vLLM
fork with a block-masked trainer "so that the gradient computation matches the masked generation pattern"; Sparse-RL
2601.10079 (January 2026), Shadow Mask Distillation 2605.06850 (May) and Sparrow 2606.08446 (June) state the
sampler-under-compressed-cache versus learner-under-dense-context mismatch as their premise. The claim's pre-registered
surprise (the ghost channel is consumed; the divergence is at least ten times the numerical floor) is the field's stated
knowledge: Gate 2B. Density on the trainer half is about twelve on-target papers in twelve months (Gate 6), the incumbents
are shipped code (a vLLM fork, a Slime recipe, LEGO-RL's recompute-under-compaction), and the exact question — rollouts
under eviction, gradient bias of text recompute measured — is unasked because the exact fix costs one forward with a mask,
which is cheaper than measuring the bias it removes. What is not in print is a footnote: Memento's restart ablation on a
model never trained for compaction, under a harness-level drop of tool outputs (AKV's K = 12), on a tool-agent benchmark.
It is kept as an optional one-day step attached to CL-W43-12's KV-residency lever, not as a claim.

## 3. The two closest papers' own limitation text (grep-verified)

- MemoryWalker 2609.00865, §7: "Future work includes extending the framework to broader compression policies (e.g.,
  dropping tool observations or using latent summaries), testing across a broader range of harnesses, and adapting exact
  tree-consistent replay to sparse and linear-attention architectures." And §4.3 on the exact arm's requirement: the 4D
  formulation "requires more larger attention-mask and white-box access to both the harness and the model", with
  "white-box access to eviction records". Its measured gap: "Under eviction-heavy compression, the Naive-Compressed
  train–inference log-probability gap reaches 0.366 on AgentFold, ≈26× the no-compression floor of 0.014 (§ 6)."
- Memento 2604.09852, §7: "Looking forward, we see two natural extensions: scaling the RL recipe to larger models, and
  applying Memento to long-horizon agent tasks where agent steps form natural blocks and context windows are the primary
  bottleneck." §6.2.1: "Recomputing memento KVs without block access drops AIME24 accuracy from 66.1% to 50.8%"; "Memento
  retains the KV cache, and this retention is critical." App. A.2.3: "During RL rollouts, block masking must be active so that
  the policy generates under the same conditions as deployment"; "Training forward passes use a corresponding sparse
  block-masked attention implementation so that the gradient computation matches the masked generation pattern."
- Third, for the serving half's mechanism as stated knowledge, Context Language Models 2609.37725 App. B: "Because the
  reused states were computed under the pre-edit context, Suffix Cache Reuse approximates re-prefilling"; and its related
  work on Memento: it "finds that these states retain useful information from the evicted block."

So "dropping tool observations" is the one cell both closest papers leave open, in their own words — and it is a cell, not a
claim: the mechanism, the ruler, the exact trainer and the serving-side measurement all exist.

## 4. Why has this not been done

The conjunction the brief asks about — an agent whose rollouts were generated under KV eviction, with the policy-gradient bias
of text recompute measured — is unasked because the exact fix is cheaper than the measurement. Once the harness exposes
eviction records, one forward with a 4D mask reproduces the served conditioning for every token (MemoryWalker Theorem 1,
dense attention; Memento's block mask in vLLM and in its trainer), so no stack that has the records needs the size of the bias
it would otherwise carry; the stacks without records (black-box harnesses) are MemoryWalker's SDCC case, which bounds the gap
rather than measuring the gradient. The enabling pieces landed in order: ReSum's segment rule (September 2025), Sparse-RL's
mismatch statement for compressed caches (January 2026), Memento's engine and restart ablation (April 2026), MemoryWalker's
tree and mask (September 2026), AKV's engine (October 2026). Nothing is left for "why now" except AKV's specific drop policy
on untrained models, which is a configuration of a question already answered.

The pressure evidence routed here (pressure #11, Q08: the vLLM post's train-inference KL rising from $2\times10^{-3}$ to 29.9
with SWE-bench Pro falling from 0.56 to 0.22; the MoE routing-flip mechanism) belongs to the arithmetic and routing line —
CIS 2609.32444, DLC 2610.40945, R3/PR2/RDC — and to the proposed CL-W44-04, not to this claim; it is evidence that mismatch
matters, not that the eviction term is unmeasured.

## 10. Checklist lessons (2026-11-01)

- A claim that reads "the state is not the text" is searched first in the KV-reuse and context-compression literatures,
  whose role words are *restart*, *re-prefill*, *suffix cache reuse*, *block masking*, *conditioning consistency*,
  *eviction records*, *compressed walk*; the claim's own words ("ghost information", "operation sequence") returned 0–1 hits
  while the role words returned the occupants (Q8 = 1, Q19 = 1, Q20 = 10 with Memento inside).
- A systems paper's "we treat X as a feature" (AKV on retained KV) is checked against the papers it is about to be compared
  with: the test it skipped had been run six months earlier on another unit of eviction (Memento, reasoning blocks).
- When the fix for a mismatch is exact and costs one forward (a mask from records), the bias it removes is not a measurement
  anyone funds; a probe that prices the bias must name a consumer who cannot apply the mask (black-box harnesses, linear
  attention) or it is a footnote.
- The surviving cell of two closest papers' future-work sentences ("dropping tool observations"; "long-horizon agent tasks")
  is a configuration of their result, not a claim, unless the cell's expected reading differs from theirs in sign.
