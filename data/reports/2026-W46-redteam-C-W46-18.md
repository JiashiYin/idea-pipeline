Glossary (each term once; the record carries the full list). **Score**: Genie Sim's task metric, $\mathrm{Score} = \frac{1}{nS}\sum_i\sum_s c_{is}$ over $n$ episodes and $S$ scored stages with stage credit $c_{is} \in [0,1]$; the per-episode score is $c_i = \frac{1}{S}\sum_s c_{is}$. **Physics step** $h$: the PhysX integration interval; shipped $h_0 = 1/120$ s. **Control tick** $1/f_c$ with $f_c = 30$ Hz; **render rate** 60 Hz. **Contact offset** $d_c$: the per-shape distance at which contacts are generated before touch. **Solver iterations** $(N_p, N_v)$: TGS position / velocity passes per step. **Inference pause** $P$: `pause_physics_during_inference`, shipped off. **Paired shift** $\delta_{t,k} = \mathrm{Score}_t(\theta_k) - \mathrm{Score}_t(\theta_0)$ on the same layouts, with $\widehat{\mathrm{SE}} = \mathrm{sd}(c_i^{(k)} - c_i^{(0)})/\sqrt{n}$. **Harness repeat floor** $f_t = 1.96\,\hat\sigma_{d,t}/\sqrt{n}$ from identical-setting repeats. **Mover**: a task with $|\hat\delta_{t,k}| > 0.10$ and the paired 95% interval excluding zero. **Exploit cell**: $J_{\theta}(\pi_{\theta_0}) - J_{\theta_0}(\pi_{\theta_0})$ for a policy trained on data generated at $\theta_0$.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Red-teamed (survives, narrowed).** 20 objections, 7 killing and 13 limiting; 16 answered, 4 conceded, 0 open. No killing objection stands unanswered, so the probe is not killed; four concessions rewrite it. (1) The filed mover rule double-counted the noise (the repeat floor is itself a 95% band): a true 15-point shift fired in 4-23% of simulated replicates and the kill fired with probability 0.76-0.99 when three tasks truly shifted 15 points — a Gate 1 failure, replaced by an interval-excludes-zero rule with the repeat pairs as a negative control (power 0.79-0.85 at 15 points, graded scores, $n = 100$). (2) "Solver iterations leave them flat" is an equivalence at 10 points that no affordable $n$ establishes (probability 0.00-0.10 at a true zero, $n = 100$); it becomes a bounded across-task mean (07c). (3) The only released policies are the Season I pi0.5 baselines, trained on the release's own synthetic suites (ACoT README), so the solo probe measures the exploit cell of a sim-trained board entry, not the sensitivity of the real-trained policies behind the validity number; the headline names the policy and the register (boards rank sim-trained entries beside the README's "<10%" footnote); the real-trained reading is the lab arm. (4) No swept task is a Table B1 pair (the 22 pairs run on G1 Omnipicker, G2 Swiftpicker, G2 Wuji and Arx X5; the baselines run the G2 Omnipicker boards), so "recompute MAE and $r$ against the real column" is removed and the README's sentence is the object. Two release-file findings changed the probe before the attack: 1 kHz is not commensurate with the 30 Hz tick and 60 Hz render (refinement set 240 and 960 Hz), and the shipped `pause_physics_during_inference = False` makes simulated time per inference call a wall-clock quantity (primary arms run the pause on). Grade of the claim after this red team: **proper (narrow, weak)**; cost 120-150 GPU-h (ceiling 168 by a written cut list), about $200-300 or $0 owned, 14 days.

## 1. The design under attack

**Statement (v2).** On the released Genie Sim Benchmark (Isaac Sim 6.2 image), with the released RoboColiseum Season I pi0.5 baselines (`instruction_and_robust_pi05`, `manipulation_pi05`, `spatial_pi05`; ModelScope) served through the ACoT inference server, choose 8 contact-rich Isaac-PhysX board tasks on the G2 Omnipicker by a written rule (a grasp-and-transport, insertion, stacking, drawer or handover stage; geometric checkers only, no VLM-graded stage; pilot Score in $[0.10, 0.90]$); hold the control tick $1/30$ s, render 60 Hz, CPU PhysX and $P$ on; evaluate each task for $n = 100$ fixed-layout episodes at the shipped setting $\theta_0$ three times and at four one-at-a-time changes — $h_1 = 1/240$ s, $h_2 = 1/960$ s, $2d_c$ on task objects and gripper pads, $(N_p, N_v) = (64, 16)$ — plus one calibration cell at $\theta_0$ with the shipped $P$ off ($n = 50$). Report $\hat\delta_{t,k}$ with its paired interval and $f_t$ per task and knob, the convergence pattern $|\mathrm{Score}(h_2) - \mathrm{Score}(h_1)| < |\mathrm{Score}(h_1) - \mathrm{Score}(h_0)|$, the inference-pause shift, and beside them the README's "<10%" and Table B1's 22 rows.

**Clauses and kills.** 07a (step): refining to 240 or 960 Hz moves at least 3 of 8 tasks; dead if fewer than 2 move under both refinements and none has $|\hat\delta| > 0.10$. 07b (offset): $2d_c$ moves at least 3 of 8; dead at fewer than 2 and none above 0.10. The claim is dead when 07a and 07b are both dead. 07c (control): $(64, 16)$ moves fewer than 2 and the across-task mean $\overline{|\hat\delta|} < 0.05$; its death is a finding (the shipped iteration count is unconverged), not a kill. Readout 07d (protocol, not a clause): $P$ off versus on at $\theta_0$. Guard: the three repeat pairs must yield zero movers or the mover rule is tightened to the floor. Parked at step 0 if fewer than 6 qualifying tasks exist under the baselines, if `--app.physics_step` is not honoured on the live stage, or if the policy loop steps a fixed count per tick and cannot be made time-based in one line.

**Cost.** Step 0 (2 days, about 2 GPU-h), pilot (about 6 GPU-h), sweep in priority order (R1, 240, 960 at $n = 100$; R2; $2d_c$; (64, 16); $P$ off at $n = 50$; R3 at $n = 50$) cut from the bottom to hold 168 GPU-h; central 120-150 GPU-h on one 48 GB GPU hosting Isaac and the pi0.5 server; episode wall time a judgment (60-90 s at 120 Hz, +10% / +35%) re-costed by the pilot.

## 5. Verdict and the claim's grade

**Probe C-W46-18 v2: red-teamed.** **Claim CL-W46-07: proper (narrow, weak)**, status open, headline v2 as in the title. Gate 0: passes after the rewrite — premise numbers fetched and dated with their verbs; conditioning set written (checkpoint as served, layouts, setting pair, engine build, $P$ on); the quantity is the exploit cell for the named policy and the headline says so; plain trick exists. Gate 1: passes — 120-150 GPU-h with a 168 ceiling enforced by the cut list, kill written, power computed under the design's own noise model, both outcomes informative. Gate 2: Route B, narrowly — the engine-comparison statement (coarse outcomes step-robust) and the sim-to-sim statement (step decisive for a tuned policy) disagree, neither has the number for a VLA Score on a validated benchmark, and the validity community reports no sensitivity. Gate 3: one community (benchmark owners, validity-table authors, reviewers) — proper, not golden; the consequence is a reporting rule. Gate 4: mechanism (per-step penetration and contact generation scale with $h$ and $d_c$; a policy tuned to $h_0$ has margins set by $h_0$ force histories; the iteration count is converged at $m \ge 4$ substeps) and consequences written both ways, with a refuting observation for each prediction. Gate 5: the rule is durable; the numbers expire with the next release; lab-adjacent. Gate 6: concept-level kill search survived (2026-11-15); race window 4-8 weeks against 14 days — adequate for proper, borderline for golden. Position clause: solo is the board register (exploit cell of the sim-trained baseline); the lab position (AgiBot / RoboColiseum: the 22 real-trained checkpoints, the real column, standing to print the row) is the version that tests the validity number itself. Weak because the register is occupied three times with other knobs, Manda's outcome-level prior predicts the null, and the solo result does not speak to real-trained policies.

## 7. Lessons for `prompts/checklist.md`

- [ ] 2026-11-15 — A mover rule that requires the shift to clear a repeat floor and a confidence band is two 95% bands stacked when the floor is itself a 95% band of a repeat difference; simulate the rule's firing rate at the headline's own effect size before pre-registering (a true 15-point shift fired in 4-23% of replicates and the kill fired on a true positive 76-99% of the time). The floor is a negative control, not a second hurdle. (C-W46-18.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — "Leaves it flat" is an equivalence claim; compute $P(|\hat\delta| + 1.96\,\mathrm{SE} < X)$ at the design's $n$ before it enters a headline (0.00-0.10 at $n = 100$ for $X = 0.10$); write a bounded across-task mean or drop the clause. (C-W46-18.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — The baseline weights a benchmark ships are trained on the benchmark's own synthetic data (ACoT README: board-to-dataset map), so a frozen-policy sweep on them measures the 2x2's exploit cell, not the sensitivity a real-validated number certifies; name the policy's training provenance in the headline and move the real-trained reading to the position that owns the checkpoints. (C-W46-18; follows the 2026-11-08 "write the 2x2's cells" lesson.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — Read the harness loop's timing knobs before any time-step arm: the shipped control tick (`fps 30`) and render rate (60) fix the admissible refinement set (1 kHz is not commensurate), and a shipped `pause_physics_during_inference = False` makes simulated time per inference call a wall-clock quantity, so the shipped protocol cannot be held fixed while $h$ changes. (C-W46-18.)
