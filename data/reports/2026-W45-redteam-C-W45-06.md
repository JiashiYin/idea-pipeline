## 0. Verdict

## 4. Lessons for `prompts/checklist.md`

- 2026-11-08 — Write the decomposition identity before a share headline, so each clause is one term of it (here $C = C_1^{\mathrm{held}} - D + [\kappa(T_{25}) - \kappa(T_{\max})]$); a clause that is not a term tests nothing about the parent.
- 2026-11-08 — A gloss of a figure ('the gap grows in $K$') is not a datum unless the plotted numbers are in the text; the defender withdrew one.
- 2026-11-08 — A power simulation of a paired design states the between-arm correlation it assumes; the attackers' independent-difficulty simulation was conservative by a factor 1.4 in SD.
- 2026-11-08 — A within-policy contrast (announced minus held) is resolved by the per-map replicate count $n$, a between-policy gap by the number of maps $M$; put the kill on the cheaper quantity where the claim allows.
- 2026-11-08 — Before writing 'method X prevents the collapse', compute the collapse ratio across the paper's own methods and sizes (GiGPO/GRPO 0.55 and 0.57; PPO/GRPO 0.82 and 0.93): a level effect is not a slope effect.
