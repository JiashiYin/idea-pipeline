**Terms (one gloss each).** *Prefix value V(h_t)*: the probability that the frozen agent, restarted from its own state
after step t with the remaining budget, finishes the task; estimated by executing K fresh continuations (V̂).
*V-curve*: V̂ over t for one failed run; *cliff* (one large drop) or *erosion* (many small drops). *Anchored in-context
critic (LVRL)*: a frontier model asked for the value of every step of a finished run, told the outcome and the group
pass rate μ_G, shown k sibling summaries, with step 0 pinned to μ_G and the last step to the outcome after prediction.
*Value captured VC_k*: the share of a run's total lost value that lies within k steps of a proposed step, eq. (9) of
2610.17633: VC_k(m) = max(0, V(h_{m−1−k}) − V(h_{m+k})) / (V(h_0) − V(h_T)). *Marginal-matched shuffle*: the critic's
interior values permuted within the trajectory. *Linear decay* V_lin(t) = μ_G·(1 − t/T): the straight line between the
two anchors, what GRPO's uniform split implies. *Hindsight smoother vs forward filter*: a reader of the whole
transcript and outcome vs an estimator that sees only the prefix. *Budget deficit* D_t = V_B(h_t) − V_{B−t}(h_t): the
part of a late drop that is the shrinking step budget, not the state. *Doomed curve*: p(x) ≤ 1/8, "nothing to
localize" (2610.17633 Def. 4). *Decided pairs*: pairs of states whose executed pass rates differ (2610.21088's
resolvable-pairs row).

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed as designed; claim CL-W43-06 parked with a written re-open trigger.** Two objections attack the claim and were
not answered after two rounds. (i) The claim's null is degenerate: for a localizer scored by VC_k, "beats its own
marginal-matched shuffle" is identical to "beats a uniformly random step" (the interior transitions are exchangeable
under the permutation, so the shuffle's expected VC_k is Table 32's random-step row, already cited); for Spearman on
levels, any non-increasing curve — linear decay, the anchors alone — beats a shuffle by construction and scores 0.86
on erosion curves, above the ground truth's own split-half ceiling of 0.61 (T-O1, S-O1). The only null that separates
step-reading from anchor-reading is linear decay between the critic's own anchors, or an outcome-blind critic; the
headline names neither and `kills_if` fires on neither. (ii) The forward half of the claim's substance is in print as of
2026-10-15: Probe-and-Resume 2610.21088 (trick: ask a frontier critic for P(success | prefix) at 25/50/75% of a
terminal-agent trajectory, grade it against executed continuations, find it at chance early, and so invoke the critic
only when the agent says it is done) reports within-task pairwise accuracy 49–56% at 25% depth (chance 50%), 56–62% at
50%, 64–69% at 75%, with bootstrap intervals over tasks, a decided-pairs split and a best-constant row — on 1,111
intermediate states of 89 Terminal-Bench 2.1 tasks (P-O1). The record's pre-registered "expected" result (critic at
chance early, 2–3× random late) is therefore a published finding and Gate 2B reads "service" (P-O3); the kill search
missed it because the lane's vocabulary ("credit assignment", "process reward", "placebo", "Monte Carlo") does not
contain "test-time search", "critic", "intermediate states", "continuation pass rate" (P-O6). Around those two, the
design does not hold: the wrong-outcome placebo on an all-failed set forces a non-decreasing curve, so its kill clause
cannot fire (S-O4); the "dollar-matched K = 4" arm is contaminated by the truth's own samples and matched dollars buy
11–290 continuations (S-O3); the critic is a hindsight smoother ("a hindsight estimate rather than a true state-value
function", LVRL §3.2), so no forward or restart-controller consequence follows from grading it (T-O2); 61 of 107 curves
sit in the budget-capped strata where the owners say part of the late drop is the clock (T-O3); the kill is a one-sided
conjunction with SE ≈ 0.04–0.05 on VC_2 means (T-O4); six curves are doomed (T-O5); one prompt, one vendor and one
scaffold cannot license "published step-credit critics" (T-O6). What survives is a different probe: the forward
frontier arm on the K = 32 curves — the same model shown only the prefix — beside linear decay and the owners' restart
gain, as a replication note answering 2610.21088's own label-noise caveat (5 continuations per state, SE up to 22
points); about $300–900 in API calls, zero GPU-hours, two days, news only if it reverses the published early null
(P-O2). Raised 20 objections; 18 conceded, 0 answered, 2 open.

### T-O1 — the shuffle is a degenerate null; the headline tests the wrong null and `kills_if` never uses the right one (OPEN → kill)
- **Objection.** Let c = (c_0, …, c_T) be the critic's curve on a failed run with c_0 = μ_G and c_T = 0 fixed by
  anchoring. The marginal-matched shuffle permutes the interior values by a uniform σ; the localizer is
  m(c) = argmax_t (c_{t−1} − c_t) over interior transitions. Under σ the interior transitions are exchangeable, so
  P(m(c∘σ) = t) is uniform and E[VC_k(m(c∘σ))] = E_{t~Unif}[VC_k(t)] — exactly Table 32's random-step row (VC1 0.04,
  VC3 0.10 on 47 curves). For Spearman against the isotonic (monotone non-increasing) fit, any non-increasing c has
  ρ = 1 up to ties and the shuffle has E[ρ] = 0, so "beats shuffle" holds for linear decay, for the anchors alone and for
  the critic alike. The only arms that separate "reads step evidence" from "reads the two anchors" are V_lin and an
  outcome-blind, anchor-free critic; the record has V_lin as an arm but the headline and `kills_if` never test against
  it. On these curves the decisive step is early (median t*/T = 0.28; 25 of 47 in the first third, 40 of 47 in the first
  two thirds, Table 9), so "first two thirds" is where nearly all the loss lies.
- **Evidence.** 2610.15840 §3.2 [full]: "We anchor the endpoints to empirical data: the initial state to the group
  success rate (Vϕ(si,0) = µG) and the terminal state to the observed final reward (Vϕ(si,Ti) = R(τi))"; 2610.17633 §4
  and §6.8 eq. (9) [full]; [argument]; `theory_tstar.py` counts.
- **Generator's answer.** Conceded: the headline must read "do not beat the linear decay between their own anchors" and
  `kills_if` must be written on VC_2(critic) − VC_2(V_lin) and on increments; the generator may not change the claim
  mid-red-team. **Status: open** (attacks the claim as worded).

### T-O2 — the critic is a hindsight smoother; the audit's ceiling and `would_change` infer a forward filter from it (conceded)
- **Objection.** V(h_t) conditions on h_t; the critic's value at t conditions on the whole transcript h_T and the outcome,
  c_t = f(h_T, R, μ_G, siblings). A hindsight reader locates t* by reading backward from the symptom at t_sym > t*
  (2610.17633 §6.4: the decisive step "is usually the step at which the agent adopts a wrong diagnosis, often an
  innocuous read or a probe script", symptoms come later); a forward estimator cannot. So VC_k(critic) high is consistent
  with zero forward information, and the ceiling scenario ("a frontier model carries forward-looking value … usable by
  restart controllers") does not follow.
- **Evidence.** 2610.15840 §3.2 [full]: "However, Vϕ is conditioned on the completed trajectory and its outcome, so it is
  a hindsight estimate rather than a true state-value function."; §4.4: "the frozen LLM critic's value estimates are not
  strictly on-policy"; 2610.17633 Def. 1 ("V(h_t) = Pr(Y = 1 | h_t, π, budget B − t)"), §6.4 [full]; 2608.19760 §5
  ("knowing how the trajectory ended changes the score, but not toward what each step contributed") [full].
- **Generator's answer.** Conceded: the forward consequence needs a prefix-only frontier arm (P-O2). **Status: conceded.**

## 7. Checklist lessons (2026-10-20, red team)

- A marginal-matched shuffle of a monotone, endpoint-anchored value curve is the random-step baseline: for a localizer
  it has the random row's expected value, and for a rank correlation any monotone curve beats it. The null that tests
  step-reading in an anchored critic is the straight line between its own anchors (linear decay) or an outcome-blind
  version of the same prompt; write the headline against that null.
- A critic that reads the whole transcript and the outcome is a hindsight smoother, not a value function (LVRL says so);
  nothing measured on it licenses a forward consequence (restart controllers, distillation targets). The forward arm
  (prefix only, no outcome) is a separate, cheaper experiment and must be priced on its own.
- A placebo must be able to move the measured statistic in both directions: on an all-failed set, "told success" with
  an outcome anchor forces a non-decreasing curve, so "the placebo leaves the largest drop unchanged" cannot fire.
  Check each kill clause for satisfiability before pre-registering a conjunction.
- Rank correlation against a monotone ground truth is shape-dominated: simulate the split-half ceiling and the
  trivial-baseline score (linear decay) under the release's own schedule before promising a Spearman gap; grade
  increments or value captured instead.
- "Dollar-matched" needs the unit (per call or per trajectory bill), the GPU price and the continuation cost from the
  release's own stated collection cost; subsampling the truth's continuations contaminates the arm.
- Budget is part of the state in executed-prefix ground truth (V is relative to the remaining budget); run the primary
  analysis on the uncapped strata the owners themselves use.
- The lane's kill vocabulary ("credit assignment", "process reward", "Monte Carlo", "placebo") missed a five-day-old
  audit written in test-time-search words ("critic", "intermediate states", "continuation pass rate", MCTS, fork);
  density and kill queries for L2 need both vocabularies (third occurrence of the vocabulary lesson: 2026-10-08,
  2026-10-16, today).
