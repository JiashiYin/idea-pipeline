Glossary (each term once): **compaction** — replacing an agent's earlier context with a model-written summary when the window fills.
**Writer / compressor** — the model call that writes the summary. **Successor** — the agent that continues from the summary. **Boundary** —
one compaction event; the history just before it is the writer's input. **Paired replay** — continuing the same restored environment state
once from the full history (PRE) and once from the summary (POST); the harm is POST minus PRE. **Burden** — TRACE's count of blocked or
repeated tool calls among the next $K$ actions. **Gauge study** — metrology's design for an instrument: repeatability (same artifact, same
gauge, repeated readings), reproducibility (different operators or gauges), bias against a reference. **Writer-stage repeatability** — how
much the harm changes when the same history is re-compacted. **Successor-stage repeatability** — how much it changes when the same summary
is continued again. **Variance component** — the part of a total spread attributable to one level of a nested design. **Stable damage** —
Compact-Bench's label for a repair that fails under the summary in at least two of three independent (summary, continuation) draws.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed, both positions (Gate 0 premise false; Gate 6 "why not done" = no reason found; the design is in print).** The claim's second
clause — that the lane's harm labels are single readings of an instrument "whose repeatability has never been measured" — is wrong in
three fetched places. (i) TRACE itself, in both versions (2608.06503v1 of 2026-08-06 and v2 of 2026-10-14), re-compacts the same history:
"The frozen base compressor independently generates three candidate summaries" $s_{b,n} = S(I_b; P_0)$, $n \in \{1,2,3\}$ (Eq. 5), scores
each by the paired burden with the PRE continuations shared, and uses the best-minus-worst spread across the three re-draws as its training
signal — the writer-stage spread is TRACE's optimizer, so it exists and was exploited, though its variance component was never reported.
(ii) Compact-Bench's stable-damage label is a re-draw rule: backbones "resample the summary and POST while reusing the first-seed PRE
baseline" over three seeds, and "Repeated runs confirm 120 of the 126 first-round damaged instance–backbone pairs" — 95% of harm labels
recur when the summary is re-drawn together with the continuation, which is the field's stated knowledge on exactly the claim's quantity:
"most observed damage recurs across independent samples". (iii) Parallel compaction 2605.23296 ran the summary-level gauge study: "run
compaction ten times under identical prompt and input conditions with temperature-driven sampling", finding length coefficients of
variation 16–85% and pairwise cosine similarity 0.47–0.86 across three backbones and 4k–96k inputs, and writing the mechanism the claim
calls its own ("downstream QA accuracy will vary between runs not because the model reasoned differently, but because it received a
different context"). The import read TRACE at abstract level and missed Eq. 5 (a fourth occurrence of "a hit counted and unopened is a
miss"); it read 2605.23296's abstract and underweighted its Section 5.2. What is unpublished is only the number: the writer component
$\sigma^2_W$ as a share of harm variance. Three groups hold the data to compute it today (TRACE's three candidates per boundary with
burdens; Compact-Bench's 126 x 3 re-draws; 2605.23296's ten draws), the first two are active in the lane (TRACE v1 August, PAIR September,
TRACE v2 October; Compact-Bench v2 October), and none has a stated reason not to — "why not done" is "no reason found", the red flag. The
claim's headline quantity is also mis-specified (Gate 0): with three continuations per summary the successor component $\sigma^2_E$
dominates the denominator (TRACE's split-half correlation is 0.40), so a total-variance writer share under 0.10 does not license the
claim's own `if_false` reading "the compressor is effectively deterministic"; the identified quantity is $\sigma^2_W / (\sigma^2_B +
\sigma^2_W)$, the writer's share of the variance of expected harm, which is what caps a history-based trigger. And the "deployed sampling
setting" the headline names is unstated in three of the four lane papers it cites (TRACE: Ollama, no temperature in the text; SJC: official
APIs, none; Compact-Bench: "temperature and other decoding settings are the service defaults"); only 2611.06545 states it (Qwen3 0.6,
gpt-oss-20b 1.0). A narrow residue survives as a question to ask, not a claim to pursue: whether re-compaction flips the harm sign at the
boundaries where the successor replicate agrees with itself — answerable from TRACE's Eq. 5 artifacts by email before any GPU-hour.

## 5. Why not done, and why now

The writer-stage re-draw is done as a method (TRACE Eq. 5, with burdens, at 12 training boundaries), as a label rule (Compact-Bench, 126
cells x 3 seeds, writer and successor re-drawn together) and as a summary-text measurement (2605.23296, ten draws). The variance component
is not reported by any of them because none needed it: TRACE wanted the extreme pair, Compact-Bench a stability filter, 2605.23296 a serving
argument. There is no enabling piece missing — the data sit in three groups' artifacts, the nested estimator is textbook (NIST/SEMATECH
2.4.4), and TRACE's group published three lane papers in ten weeks. "Why not done" is therefore "no reason found", and the W42 lesson
applies: the field's standard answer (gauge R&R for agent scores: 2610.00651, 2608.11323, 2512.06710; for judges: 2609.27787, 2609.29709;
for two-stage writer-reader pipelines: 2607.19386) has been imported everywhere but the compaction boundary, and the compaction groups
already hold the re-draws. "Why now" for the solo probe (2611.06545's harness, 2026-11-04) is real but the owners' why-now is older.

## 6. Gate-by-gate verdict

- **Gate 0 (premise hygiene): fails.** "Repeatability has never been measured" is false for the summary (2605.23296, ten draws) and for
  the harm label under joint re-draw (Compact-Bench, 120 of 126); "one summary per boundary" is false for TRACE's method (Eq. 5). The
  headline quantity — $\sigma^2_W / (\sigma^2_B + \sigma^2_W + \sigma^2_E)$ with $n = 3$ continuations — is not the quantity the sentence
  interprets: with the successor's half-half correlation at 0.40 (2610.08722), $\sigma^2_E$ is of the order of $\sigma^2_B + \sigma^2_W$,
  so a total share under 0.10 can hold with a writer that is far from deterministic, and the `if_false` reading ("effectively
  deterministic", "single-summary labels are licensed") does not follow. The identified quantity is $\sigma^2_W / (\sigma^2_B +
  \sigma^2_W)$, the writer's share of the variance of expected harm $\mu_{bj} = \mu + \alpha_b + \beta_{j(b)}$, which is exactly what
  bounds a history-based predictor. The "deployed sampling setting" is unstated in three of four cited papers (section 4), so the headline
  names a protocol level it cannot name (2026-11-01 lesson on design-dependent shares).
- **Gate 1 (cheap to kill): passes** as costed (30 GPU-hours, 10 days on 2611.06545's harness) — and passes more cheaply by email: TRACE's
  Eq. 5 artifacts (three summaries x burdens x 12 boundaries) and Compact-Bench's Table 7 answer the sign-flip question at zero cost.
- **Gate 2 (pressure): fails on route B.** The pre-registered surprise (writer share at least 0.25) is written against "one summary per
  boundary is the summary", but the field has stated both ends of the quantity: the summary text varies on every call (2605.23296) and the
  harm label mostly does not under joint re-draw (Compact-Bench, 95% recurrence). The claim's expected result contradicts the only re-draw
  measurement in print and the record does not answer it in writing; the claim's negation is 2605.23296's own sentence. Route A: the
  corpus study's "attenuation reading" and "more replays" are one source type (a paper's own limitation), not two.
- **Gate 3 (consumers): would pass** — every compaction evaluator would add a writer-replicate arm — but the consumers already run re-draws
  (TRACE, Compact-Bench) without reporting the component; the action change is "report a number you have", a reporting line, not a result.
- **Gate 4 (mechanism and consequence): passes** as written; the mechanism is 2605.23296's sentence.
- **Gate 5 (durability): passes**; a gauge study applies to any compressor.
- **Gate 6 (ownership, density, timing): fails.** Occupied in design by TRACE (Eq. 5) and in label rule by Compact-Bench; the component is a
  one-line addition to data three groups hold; "why not done" is "no reason found"; lane rate about 3.4 papers per week against a 10-day
  probe, and the specific owners can publish the number in their next version. Pre-window precedents named: BRIO 2203.16804 and
  SummaReranker 2203.06569 (candidate summaries as draws to rank), USC 2311.17311, the NIST/SEMATECH gauge-study design (ch. 2.4).
- **Position clause.** *Solo*: killed — the premise is false, the owners hold the data, and the solo probe would publish a number TRACE's
  group can print from disk. *Lab*: killed as a claim — in a room that owns a compaction harness, the writer share is a day's analysis on
  re-draws the harness already produces (TRACE Eq. 5 is the recipe), and by the claim's own `if_false` register the likely answer is a
  protocol footnote; the lab-useful artifact is the reporting line (declare the compressor's temperature; report $m$ and the writer
  component), which belongs in the W44 pressure file, not in a claim.
