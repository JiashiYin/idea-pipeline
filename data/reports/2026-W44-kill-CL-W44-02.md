Glossary (each term once). *Re-grade*: rescore saved trajectories under a changed verifier, no new rollouts.
*Re-run*: new rollouts on the changed task. *DiD* (difference-in-differences): the repaired-task delta minus the
untouched-task delta, so drift that touches every task cancels. *Random-subset ceiling*: the spread of rank
correlations between model orderings on a random 19-task subset and its 47-task complement, drawn many times from the
same matrix; a repaired-versus-untouched disagreement is news only beyond it. *Composition term*: the part of a version
delta that comes from tasks removed or added, not from tasks kept. *Matched-random subtest* (2609.00482's word): a
random subset drawn to match the target subset's size and difficulty strata.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Survived-kill, weak.** No fetched neighbour decomposes a shipped version pair of an agent benchmark into its
repaired and untouched tasks, and none compares the model ordering on repaired tasks with the ordering on untouched
tasks against a random-subset ceiling. The mechanism, though, is occupied at the *filter* level in the same benchmark
family: Automated Benchmark Auditing (2605.26079, May 2026) recomputes the public Terminal-Bench 2 and SWE-bench
Verified leaderboards with audit-flagged tasks removed, uses the Terminal-Bench 2 maintainers' own fix PR #53 (22
tasks) as its ground truth, and reports "Terminal-Bench 2 reshuffles more aggressively than swe_bench_verified: a
non-trivial share of the public task set is flagged, lifting per-model scores by several percentage points and moving
multiple submissions by three or more rank positions"; the text-to-SQL audit (2601.08778, Jan 2026) runs the paired
original-versus-corrected design on 16 agents with per-item flip tables by correction type and a 0.32 rank correlation.
What those designs cannot say, and what the claim says, is how a *re-run* under the owner's recalibration (a flat
8-hour timeout and CPU/memory changes on every task) splits between the 19 fixed tasks and the 47 others — in a
re-grade or a filter the untouched tasks cannot move, so the "repair share" is one by construction, and the claim has
content only in the full re-run that Terminal-Bench 4.0 actually required ("breaking changes that require re-running
trials"). The field's stated expectation is split three ways (ABA: broken tasks are the lift and the reshuffle; the
Terminal-Bench team: "TB 4.0 has fewer agent timeouts and errors than 3.0, reducing measurement noise", i.e. the
recalibration is the change; contamination's analogue 2609.02899: inflation without reordering, $\rho = 0.997$), so
either outcome of the first clause moves a stated belief. Survival is weak for three reasons: it is one version pair of
one benchmark; the owner's own protocol post already specifies the per-task diff machinery ("reuse, regrade, and rerun
trials as necessary, in that order") and the owner is the obvious author of the number at 5.0; and the ordering clause
as written fires on difficulty composition unless its null is a matched-random subtest, which is a redesign, not a
detail. Gate 6 passes from the solo position (public dataset revisions, about $1–1.5k of API spend, a two-to-three-week
desk job) with the race against the owner inside one release cycle; from a lab position the decomposition is a day on
owned paired logs. The grade stays *proper, not golden* (Gate 3: one community must change something).

## 4. Gate 0 note the search produced

The claim's first clause is a statement about a *re-run*. Under the owner's own protocol post, untouched tasks are
re-used, minor bumps re-graded and only major bumps re-run, so between any two versions managed that way the kept-task
delta on untouched tasks is zero by construction and the repair share is $1$; the 2601.08778, UTBoost and ABA designs
share that property. Terminal-Bench 4.0 is the exception the claim needs: the recalibration (8-hour flat timeout, CPU
and memory) was "breaking changes that require re-running trials" on every task, so the 47 untouched tasks carry a real
delta (recalibration plus run noise) against which the 19 repaired tasks' delta is compared. Write the quantity as
$$S_{\text{repair}} = \frac{\big(\bar\Delta_{R} - \bar\Delta_{U}\big)\, n_R}{\Delta_{\text{kept}}}, \qquad
\Delta_{\text{kept}} = \sum_{t \in R \cup U} w_t\,\big(p_t^{4.0} - p_t^{3.0}\big),$$
with $R$ the 19 repaired tasks, $U$ the 47 untouched, $n_R = 19$, $\bar\Delta_{R}$ and $\bar\Delta_{U}$ the mean
per-task pass-rate changes on each set, $p_t^{v}$ the task's pass rate under version $v$ for the fixed model and pinned
harness, and $w_t$ the task weight (uniform on the board). The denominator must be the kept-task delta, with the
composition term from the 8 removed tasks reported beside it, and the run-noise floor for $\bar\Delta_{U}$ comes from
identical reruns (15–36% of outcomes flip, 2610.11678; 0.83% of verdicts under rebuilt images, 2610.14460), which is
why three or more trials per cell are needed.

## 5. Why not done

An enabling piece landed: Terminal-Bench 4.0 (release note 2026-08-28; board filled 2026-10-06) is the first agent
benchmark version pair with (i) a per-task changelog in each task's README, (ii) both dataset revisions persisted on one

## 9. Verdict

**survived-kill (provisional; weak).** Reason: no neighbour occupies the shipped-version-pair decomposition or the

**Narrowest surviving headline.** On Terminal-Bench 3.0 → 4.0, with the model fixed and the harness pinned, the 19
repaired tasks carry more than half of the kept-task delta after the recalibration delta on the 47 untouched tasks is
subtracted ($S_{\text{repair}} > 0.5$, task-cluster bootstrap), and the model ordering on the 19 repaired tasks departs
from the ordering on the 47 untouched tasks beyond a difficulty-matched random-subset ceiling.

**Probe recommendation.** `P-W44-02-repair` is now the cheapest probe and stands, with three redesign notes written
with the neighbours' text in hand: (1) the ordering null must be the matched-random subtest of 2609.00482 — the 19
repaired tasks were selected for flakiness and misspecification in 3.0 and sit in their own difficulty stratum, so a

reasons (timeout, OOM, output-token-exceeded) and run at least three trials per cell, because the untouched-task delta
is recalibration plus run noise and identical reruns flip 15–36% of outcomes (2610.11678); report $\bar\Delta_U$ with
and without timeout-ended trials. (3) Add a $0 re-grade arm in the ABA/text-to-SQL shape: rescore the 4.0 trajectories
of the repaired tasks under the 3.0 verifiers where only the verifier changed (the task READMEs say which), so the
verifier-fix channel is split from the instruction and environment channels before the re-run is read.
`import:2026-W44-S5xF2` is unchanged for the ordering clause but its Terminal-Bench arm sits behind the same data gate;
its matched-random floor is now shared with this probe.
