Glossary (once). *Harness / scaffold*: the fixed code around the model (prompts, tools, control loop, retries, context resets).
*Effort knob*: the vendor's reasoning-effort setting (low / medium / high / xhigh / max), which changes how many thinking tokens the
model spends per call. *Restart*: a fresh attempt from the initial state (a new session, a re-run, or the $k$-th sample in
*pass@k*, the fraction of tasks solved at least once in $k$ attempts). *Compute-matched baseline*: the plain agent given the
scaffold's spend (calls, tokens or wall-clock) with no scaffold. *Mediation share*: the fraction of a scaffold gap that a model
with spend and restart counts as covariates predicts away. *Gate 2B* (`config/standards.md`): a ruler whose expected reading equals
the field's stated knowledge is a service, not a paper.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed (Gate 2B and Gate 6).** The concept-level question — has anyone run the effort-matched or restart-matched control on a
published scaffold gain and reported what survives — is answered yes, in print, from several directions and with heterogeneous
answers, so the claim's universal "mostly" is neither open nor a surprise. The restart-matched control is a published protocol:
Harness-Delta Attribution (2610.22466, 2026-10-15) reruns the plain harness at the evolved harness's call or token budget
(pass@$k$ or majority vote), applies it to 11 executor models and three harness-evolution frameworks, and reports that
test-time scaling explains 14% of the gain on average (overfitting 54%), with per-setting splits from $O/T/G = 23/23/54$ to
$0/6/94$ on SWE-bench Verified — a published negative for "mostly restarts" on evolved harnesses and a published positive for
"mostly not design". The effort-matched control is in print on the claim's own benchmark: 2607.15439 (same author as 2610.22289)
crosses four Codex scaffolds with two effort levels on ARC-AGI-3 and reports "These gains often exceed variant differences, which
are smaller than anticipated and vary across settings"; 2610.35767 (2026-10-26) crosses six models, three reasoning efforts and
eight harnesses on root-cause analysis and finds the opposite sign — "more reasoning effort changes accuracy by a few points at
most while multiplying the time by up to seven" while the harness moves PR@1 from 16–53% to 65–77%; 2609.40303 (2026-09-30)
fixes every harness at maximum effort and an equal 24-hour budget and finds "open-source state-of-the-art harnesses provide no
advantages over a single session of a minimal-harness coding agent baseline"; 2610.27889 (2026-10-20) uses the same model at
higher effort as the reference for an optimized harness and finds "none of the selected harnesses exceeds its stronger reference
at any evaluated attempt budget" on hard tasks. The restart arm's pre-registered expectation is also contradicted on coding
agents: 2610.35971 (2026-10-26) replays matched random restarts under shared token budgets over 147,002 traces and finds
"uninterrupted continuation achieves higher solve rates at lower mean token cost than both restart policies" in 216 of 224
configurations. The field's stated knowledge since 2607.12227 (July 2026) is that matched-budget baselines are required and that
the answer depends on the benchmark (harness evolution loses to test-time scaling on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and wins by 80% on
ARC-AGI-3 games). A claim whose expected reading is "it depends, and the control is routine" is a service. Density is 16 direct
in-window neighbours, 8 of them in the last seven weeks; the race window is under one week against a 3- to 10-day first result.
**No claim-level headline survives.** What survives is one footnote-grade probe that no neighbour has run — the effort-pinned,
cross-vendor native-harness gap at fixed model on the released Trial by Trace grid (C-W44-04 step 0, $0) — which pressure
point #3 already prices as "a weekend on public boards"; it is kept as an orphan service note, not a claim.

## 3. The two closest papers' own limitation text (grep-verified)

2610.22466 (HDA), on what its compute-matched control is and is not: "The compute-matched baseline $H_0^{\mathrm{scale}}$
preserves $H_0$ while scaling it to match the average inference budget of $\hat H$. Specifically, the HDA agent compares their
inference costs using measures such as LLM calls or token usage." — "If $\hat H$ does not use more inference compute than $H_0$,
we set $H_0^{\mathrm{scale}} = H_0$." — and on the residual: "Because $\hat H^{\mathrm{neutral}}$ may still contain
unneutralized overfitting, we interpret $G$ as an upper bound on generalizable improvement rather than evidence of transfer." The
paper has no section titled Limitations; its scope is evolved harnesses, and the knob it matches is calls or tokens, never the
vendor effort setting.

2610.27889 (Elicitation or Expansion), on its scope and its effort control: "The solver's reasoning effort is fixed at medium
during harness optimization and for the base and optimized systems; the reference system uses high reasoning effort." — "Our
experimental results are bounded by the evaluated benchmarks, solver families, and optimization runs examined in this study."

Third full text, 2609.40303, on its matching and its own weakness: "Every harness runs at its backbone's maximum reasoning effort,
except MLEvolve on DeepSeek V4 Flash, which runs at medium effort" — "A fourth is possible asymmetry in tuning effort between
Malena and the external harnesses it is compared against".

Reading: the effort-knob-matched control on *hand-written* scaffolds is present (2609.40303 at max effort for all; 2607.15439 and
2610.35767 crossed), the call-matched restart control on *evolved* scaffolds is present (HDA), the pass@$k$ restart axis against
an effort reference is present (2610.27889). What no fetched paper runs is the effort-pinned, token-cut comparison of two
*vendor-native* harnesses at a fixed frontier model — the C-W44-04 instance — and 2610.04433 has already found the fixed-model
coding-harness gap to be noise plus a cap artifact on three harnesses.

## 11. Checklist lessons (2026-11-01)

- A claim seeded from a paper's "further analysis is needed" sentence is searched first in that author's own prior papers: the
  effort-notch comparison CL-W43-03 asked for was in 2607.15439 by the same author three months before the claim was seeded.
- "Restart" in a harness paper is spelled pass@$k$, "compute-matched baseline", "re-run", "continuation" and "attempt budget";
  "effort" is spelled "reference system at high reasoning effort", "matched models", "equal time budget". The claim's three nouns
  returned 1, 1 and 1 hits; the field's nouns returned the sixteen occupants.
- When two occupants report opposite signs for the same control (2609.15309 restarts win; 2610.35971 continuation wins), the
  field's stated knowledge is "benchmark-dependent" and any universal headline on that control fails Gate 2B by construction.
