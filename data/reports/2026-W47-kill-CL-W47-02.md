Glossary (each term once). **User simulator** — an LLM playing the customer against the agent so that a benchmark episode can run without a
person. **Impact** — in differential item functioning (DIF, the psychometric test of whether an item behaves differently for two matched
groups), the unmatched difference in pass rate between groups; here the shift $\lambda_g$ every agent receives when the simulator changes.
**Agent-by-simulator interaction** $\gamma_{ag}$ — the part of agent $a$'s displacement under simulator $g$ that is not shared with the other
agents; the term that can reorder a leaderboard. **Rank-moving share** $\rho$ — the fraction of the between-agent variance of the
displacement that sits above the rerun floor. **Rerun floor** $d_0$ — the per-task probability that two trials of one agent under one
simulator disagree; it sets the single-trial standard error of a score. **Double-centering** — subtracting row and column means from a cell
matrix, which removes the main effects and leaves the interaction. **Generalizability theory** — analysis of variance applied to a
measurement, with each source of variation (agent, task, judge, simulator) a facet. **Scale-stable / order-preserving** — 2609.00982's two
acceptance criteria for a replacement simulator: no agent's absolute score moves, and the agents' order does not move. **Kendall $\tau$** —
rank agreement, $+1$ for identical orders, $-1$ for reversed. **Cross-model evaluation** — the dialogue-systems protocol (Schatzmann et al.
2005) of training a policy on one simulator and testing it on another.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed at Gate 2B; Gates 4 and 6 fail behind it.** The claim's content is that a simulator change acts on a leaderboard through two
separable terms, that the order-moving term is invisible to score-level checks and the shift invisible to order-level checks, and that both

## 5. The $0 pre-registered reading, run inside the search

Setting, as in the record: agents $a$, tasks $i$, simulator groups $g \in \{r, f\}$ (gpt-4.1 at temperature 0.0; gpt-5.5 at reasoning
xhigh), one trial per cell, pass indicator $y_{aig} \in \{0, 1\}$, score $S_{ag} = |\mathcal{I}|^{-1} \sum_i y_{aig}$ in points, displacement
$\Delta_a = S_{af} - S_{ar}$, mean $\bar\Delta$ the impact. Floor: for two trials of one agent under one simulator, with per-task
discordance $d_0$,
$$\mathrm{Var}(S_{a1} - S_{a2}) = \frac{1}{n^2} \sum_i \Pr(y_{ai1} \neq y_{ai2}) = \frac{d_0}{n},$$
since $(y_{ai1} - y_{ai2})^2 = 1$ exactly on discordant tasks; so under a pure-shift null the between-agent variance of $\Delta_a$ is
$\sigma_0^2 = d_0 / n$ (in probability units, times $10^4$ in points$^2$) and the rank-moving share is
$$\rho = \max\!\left(0,\ \frac{\mathrm{Var}_a(\Delta_a) - \sigma_0^2}{\mathrm{Var}_a(\Delta_a)}\right).$$
With $d_0 = 0.196$ from the only multi-trial release (gpt-5 text agent, gpt-4.1 user, retail, 114 tasks, six pairs 0.158–0.219):
