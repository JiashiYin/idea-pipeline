Glossary (each term once). **Greedy decode** — one deterministic output per input at temperature $T = 0$. **Training temperature** —
temperature 1.0 and top-$p$ 1.0, the rollout setting Search-R1 trained under (2503.09516v5 sec. 4.3; 2505.15117 App. G.1).
**$p_x$** — the probability that one sampled four-turn episode of the fixed checkpoint on problem $x$ ends in an exact-match answer;
$\hat p_x = X_x / m$ from $m$ draws. **Band** — low ($\hat p_x \leq 0.2$), coin flip ($0.2 < \hat p_x < 0.8$), high ($\hat p_x \geq 0.8$).
**Misclassification matrix** — $\Pr[\text{band} \mid p_x, m]$ by binomial arithmetic. **Corrected share** — the band share under the
nonparametric maximum-likelihood mixing distribution of $p_x$ fitted to the counts (an inversion of the matrix without a parametric
form). **$q$** — greedy accuracy. **Coin-flip pool $A$** — the share of problems in the true coin-flip band whose greedy decode fails;
every coin-flip member of a greedy forgetting gap is in it. **Gap $G$** — problems some checkpoint's greedy decode solved and the best
checkpoint's did not; $|G| = \rho q N$, with $\rho = 0.62$ the only ratio in print (2611.12799 sec. 2: $4{,}553 / 2{,}808 - 1$, for
$U_{\geq 3}$ against the final checkpoint). **$\rho^{*} = 2A / q$** — the largest gap ratio whose coin-flip half the pool can fill.
**$\delta_0$** — exact-match disagreement between greedy decodes of the same weights under different serving configurations; at
$T = 0$ the policy has zero decision variance by construction, so $\delta_0$ is the serving stack's. **Zero-variance group** — $K$
sampled episodes of one problem that are all-correct or all-wrong (2606.10709's term).

## 7. Lessons for `prompts/checklist.md`

- [ ] 2026-11-15 — A pool bound needs the right pool: the quantity that bounds a gap's coin-flip count is the coin-flip cells whose greedy
  decode fails ($A$), not the coin-flip share ($f$); and the in-print accuracy it is scaled by must be the probed checkpoint's own row
  (0.331, Search-R1 Table 3), not another run's (0.55); the v1 line was off by 0.07 from two substitutions. (C-W46-14.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — A one-sided probe has no support reading: when the kill is an inequality in the conservative direction, the positive
  outcome is "not killed" and the record reports the break-even value of the imported parameter ($\rho^{*} = 2A / q$), not "survives".
  (C-W46-14.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — A released checkpoint is what the owners' selection rule says it is: Search-R1's is "the final checkpoint at step 600 or
  the last checkpoint prior to collapse" (2505.15117 App. G.1), not a best-validation checkpoint; read the rule before writing "the
  public checkpoint" into a transfer assumption, and read the owners' second paper (linked from the README) before the first. (C-W46-14.)
- [ ] 2026-11-15 — At fixed weights with independent seeds, a split-half agreement of binomial counts is binomial by construction; it is a
  harness calibration (RNG independence), never an informative readout; third occurrence of the identity-as-calibration lesson
  (2026-11-08, C-W45-06 objection 7; CL-W45-04). (C-W46-14.)
