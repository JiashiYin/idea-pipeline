Glossary (once). *Scaffold layer / pasted context*: a block of text the harness adds to the model's prompt that the user did not
write — a skill file (`SKILL.md`), a repository context file (`AGENTS.md`), a system instruction block, a plan, a static-analysis
export, a retrieved memory. *Placebo / sham / length-matched irrelevant control*: the same block replaced by text of the same length
(bytes, tokens or words) and format whose content is wrong, irrelevant or scrambled. *Presence effect*: the change in the agent's
outcome from the placebo block alone, $\Delta_{\mathrm{len}} = Y(\mathrm{placebo}) - Y(\mathrm{none})$. *Content effect*: the change
the true block adds beyond the placebo, $\Delta_{\mathrm{con}} = Y(\mathrm{true}) - Y(\mathrm{placebo})$. The claim's ruler is the ratio
$\rho = \Delta_{\mathrm{len}} / (\Delta_{\mathrm{len}} + \Delta_{\mathrm{con}})$; the claim says $\rho \approx 1$ and its pre-registered
surprise was $\rho \geq 0.5$. *Yield*: the agent's measured output (pass rate, confirmed findings, success). *Gate 2B*
(`config/standards.md`): a ruler whose expected reading equals the field's stated knowledge is a service, not a paper. *Gate 6*: a
headline a neighbour already owns, or a race the position cannot win.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Killed (Gate 6 and Gate 2B).** The concept-level question — has anyone run the same-length wrong-content control on a scaffold or
pasted-context effect for agents and reported presence against content — is answered yes, in print, at least ten times in the
window, and the answer is heterogeneous by model and by block. The claim's exact ruler, $\Delta_{\mathrm{len}}$ and
$\Delta_{\mathrm{con}}$ on a scaffold block pasted into a coding agent, is Table 3 of 2608.23067 (2026-08-24): a byte-matched
irrelevant `SKILL.md` ($\pm 5\%$) against the target skill on four models, giving $\rho = 0.79$ (Sonnet 4), $1.5$ (Qwen), $0.15$
(GPT-5.1), $0.30$ (DeepSeek) — the claim's surprise holds for two models and fails for two, and the paper names the two mechanisms
("length-distracted" and "content-misled") and states the claim's `if_true` consequence in its own abstract: "making length-matched
controls and per-model audits a minimum standard for Agent-Skill evaluation". The harness-layer instance is 2609.20474
(2026-09-17): a task plan against policy text shuffled to the same whitespace-word count on $\tau^2$-bench, 265 matched cells;
content wins by $7.17$ points while the sham moves success by $+1.89$ [$-3.45$, $+7.09$], and per model the sham ranges from
$-4.5$ to $+7.3$ points with one model (Kimi) whose content effect is near zero. The memory-layer instance is 2610.27714
(2026-10-20): token-matched irrelevant filler is not beaten by any of five experience-memory designs for a driving agent, and a
15-token caution line reaches the ceiling — presence wins outright. The prompt-skill instance is 2606.06454 (2026-06-04),
pre-registered with a length-matched placebo and a labels-only scaffold: the gain "track[s] scaffold structure" and the placebo
trails by 2.4 points. The data-agent instance is 2607.07504 (2026-07-08): full LLM-generated skills "perform similarly to
task-irrelevant skill-formatted content" over 7,560 + 1,512 runs. Against these, content wins in 2606.05633 (RAG rewriting:
removing the gold answer costs 28–64 F1 beyond a length-matched placebo), 2604.20911 (token-matched padding: schema content explains
62–100% of constraint dilution) and 2610.32876 (static-analysis notes in vulnerability audits: true evidence $+0.02$, false assurances
$-19$ points, direction-aware). The field's stated knowledge is therefore: the placebo effect is real, model-specific and
block-specific; run the length-matched control. A universal "as much as the right one" is contradicted in print in half the fetched
settings, and the consequence the claim would deliver is already a stated standard. Density: 13 direct in-window neighbours, 5 in the
last ten weeks. **No claim-level headline survives.** What survives is probe-grade: an exact-solve instance (C-W44-01) that nobody
has run and whose owners can run in a day, and a $0 step on a released grid (C-W44-05 step 0); both are footnotes to papers already
in print, kept as orphans only if reframed as notes on their card papers.

## 3. The two closest papers' own limitation text (grep-verified)

2608.23067 (Signal or Noise), on what its length-matched control settles and what it leaves: "Length-matched controls reveal two
failure modes: some models are length-distracted (an equally long irrelevant Skill reproduces most of the loss), while others are
content-misled (prompt length is neutral, yet Skill content still lowers Pass@2 by 1.1% to 1.4%)." — "Without C2, we would have
reported a uniform negative effect and missed that it arises from two mechanisms needing different mitigations. Future Agent-Skill
benchmarks should adopt a length-matched control as a basic requirement" — and, in Limitations: "the routing is intentionally
conservative ... so we cannot speak to what happens when Skills are deployed off-target" and "WebDev-Skills-Bench is a pre-deployment
benchmark rather than an online A/B test". On its predecessors: "Neither isolates WebDev, and neither uses a length-matched
irrelevant control to separate content from prompt-length effects" — the control was new in August 2026 for skills and is now the
paper's stated minimum bar.

2609.20474 (How Do Agent Harnesses Create Value?), on the control: "The Sham arm shuffles the domain-policy vocabulary using seed 2701
and repeats or truncates it to the Fixed whitespace-word count." — on its reading: "Sham has a positive point estimate relative to
Minimal, providing no average decrement from the shuffled control." and "DeepSeek Flash and Qwen show the largest effects; Kimi's
estimate is near zero despite a similar baseline." — and in Limitations and Generalizability: "Airline contains six tasks, and both
environments use public benchmarks, so participating models may have encountered these tasks during training. Broader tests on
held-out tasks are needed to assess transfer." and "analysis choices lack an independently timestamped preregistration."

Third full text, 2610.32876 (Trusting the Tool), on the probe C-W43-20's territory: "True evidence barely improved accuracy" and
"Future work should (i) study evidence from production analysers; (ii) extend the study to inter-procedural and agentic settings";
External validity: "inter-procedural evidence and agentic settings may behave differently."

Reading: the two closest papers have run the claim's control on two scaffold layers and report opposite leading terms (presence for
two of four models on skills; content for plans, with one model's content effect at zero and its sham at $+7.3$). Neither leaves the
claim's sentence open; both leave open the per-model, per-layer allocation of the two terms, which is a table, not a claim.

## 4. Why not done

It has been done. The enabling piece was the standardisation of the pasted block as a unit with a defined length — skills as
`SKILL.md` files (Anthropic Agent Skills, cited by 2608.23067 as deployed inside commercial coding agents) and repository context
files — which made a byte-matched irrelevant block a one-line substitution (2608.23067 §3, "workspace-aware injection"); the
template is the 2022 random-label control (2202.12837). Between April and October 2026 at least eight groups ran a length-, token-
or word-matched placebo on a scaffold, context, memory or evidence block (2604.20911, 2606.05633, 2606.06454, 2606.25449,
2607.07504, 2608.23067, 2609.20474, 2610.27714) and three more ran the adjacent matched control (2606.15017, 2610.24649,
2610.32876). The claim was seeded on 2026-10-25 from three probes whose density notes cited only the distractor literature
(2601.07226, 2609.37153); its words ("placebo", "presence", "form") are not the field's ("length-matched irrelevant control",
"sham", "token-matched filler", "length-distracted", "content-misled"). "No reason found" does not apply.

## 6. Position clause

- **Solo**: killed. The control is routine (eight groups in six months), the answer is heterogeneous and stated as such, the
  consequence ("length-matched controls ... a minimum standard") is in print, and every remaining instance is a $30–$300 footnote
  to a paper that already carries the protocol.
- **Lab**: killed as a claim. A harness or skills owner (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenCode) can run the per-model $\Delta_{\mathrm{len}}$ /
  $\Delta_{\mathrm{con}}$ audit on its own skill library in a day as product QA; that is a column on a skills marketplace listing
  (2608.23067 §5 asks for exactly this: "whether the gain survives a length-matched control"), not a paper. The exact-solve instance
  (C-W44-01) belongs to the HRP authors, who hold the solver.

## 7. Killing experiment check

The claim's cheapest probe (C-W44-01: nine placebo encodings of H5's length in the released HRP MiniWoB++ sandbox, exact soft-Bellman
ceiling per arm, about 24 GPU-hours, 5 days) is under one GPU-week and would run as written. Without the claim its result is a note
on 2610.37255's Table 10: an exact $\rho$ for one 2B model on three toy families, in a field that has already measured $\rho$ on four
frontier models (2608.23067), five chat models (2609.20474) and a driving agent (2610.27714) and found it model- and block-specific.
Neither outcome revives the claim: $\rho \geq 0.5$ in the sandbox joins the presence column (Sonnet, Qwen, driving memory, data
skills, Popperian skill); $\rho < 0.25$ joins the content column (GPT-5.1, DeepSeek, plans, RAG rewrites, constraints, static-analysis
notes). The exact-solve setting's own value — a certified ceiling with no sampling noise — is a methods footnote whose owners are
the obvious authors.

## 11. Checklist lessons (2026-11-01)

- A placebo arm is spelled "length-matched irrelevant control", "token-matched filler", "sham ... matched in word count",
  "byte-matched", "semantically matched reference run" and "conditions that differ only in the notes"; "placebo" with agent words
  returned 7 hits and one occupant, the field's spellings returned thirteen. Add these to the default density words for any
  content-versus-form claim.
- When the closest occupant has already computed the claim's ruler per model and found both signs (2608.23067 Table 3), the field's
  stated knowledge is "model-specific", and a universal headline on that ruler fails Gate 2B by construction — the same shape as
  CL-W43-03's restart clause (2609.15309 against 2610.35971).
- A "wrong-content" placebo for an evidence block is not neutral when the block makes claims: 2610.32876 shows fabricated
  assurances pull verdicts toward themselves while fabricated alarms do not. A placebo design for a pasted analysis block needs a
  content-free arm (format kept, claims removed) beside the wrong-source arm, or it measures misdirection, not presence.
- The consequence sentence of a claim ("every comparison needs arm X") is searched as a quotation before it enters `if_true`:
  2608.23067's abstract carries "length-matched controls ... a minimum standard" verbatim, two months before the claim was seeded.
