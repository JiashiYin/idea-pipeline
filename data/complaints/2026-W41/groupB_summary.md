## Complaints that appear in three or more papers

- **LLM-simulated users are too cooperative, homogeneous, patient, or error-prone, so they distort task difficulty and the reward signal.** (10 papers: 2610.09484, 2608.12253, 2608.12355, 2603.13686, 2603.04370, 2506.07982, 2502.13069, 2509.19736, 2609.38043, 2601.22607)
- **Running agentic benchmarks or long-horizon rollouts costs hundreds to tens of thousands of dollars per pass, which caps the number of trials, configurations, and ablations.** (9 papers: 2609.02783, 2609.04611, 2505.22954, 2606.10156, 2509.13305, 2506.06698, 2609.31076, 2605.31278, 2604.14624)
- **Errors made by the simulated user or by flaky tools are charged to the agent as zero reward, so the training signal cannot separate policy mistakes from environment mistakes.** (6 papers: 2601.22607, 2506.07982, 2609.38043, 2510.24701, 2509.13305, 2604.17931)
- **Human labels, human studies, and human-authored domains or skills are expensive and slow and do not scale, so most work substitutes automated proxies.** (8 papers: 2609.34374, 2605.31278, 2608.12355, 2510.24701, 2601.22607, 2506.07982, 2609.31076, 2610.09484)
- **RL training collapses: policy entropy, output diversity, or output format degrade under GRPO-style training (simulator collapse, diversity collapse, format collapse, template collapse, sparse-reward collapse).** (6 papers: 2608.12253, 2610.00991, 2510.24701, 2509.13305, 2604.14624, 2509.19736)
- **LLM-as-judge rewards and evaluations are biased, non-deterministic, or too expensive to run at every step.** (6 papers: 2609.34374, 2606.10156, 2605.31278, 2609.02783, 2604.14624, 2609.38043)
- **Transfer of simulator-trained agents to real humans is untested, and held-out evaluation uses other LLM simulators that may share the same biases.** (4 papers: 2610.09484, 2608.12253, 2603.13686, 2609.38043)
- **Turn-level credit assignment in multi-turn RL is unsolved: dense per-turn rewards or heuristics misattribute credit and can make training worse than sparse outcome rewards.** (4 papers: 2604.02869, 2509.19736, 2604.14624, 2607.21856)
- **Live web or API tools during RL are costly, rate-limited, slow, and non-deterministic, forcing offline or simulated replacements.** (3 papers: 2604.17931, 2510.24701, 2509.13305)
- **Too few trials per task (often one) because of cost, so per-task variance is uncharacterized and models with overlapping intervals cannot be distinguished.** (4 papers: 2609.04611, 2606.10156, 2608.10357, 2609.38043)
- **Context length or context management limits long-horizon agents and small models in particular.** (3 papers: 2510.24701, 2604.17931, 2603.04370)
- **Results are confined to one domain, one algorithm, or small models, with generalization unverified.** (6 papers: 2604.02869, 2610.00991, 2606.10156, 2607.21856, 2608.12253, 2603.13686)

Appeared in only two papers but worth tracking: contamination or memorization of public benchmark domains (2609.04611, 2606.10156); refusal or connection failures of closed-API models used as simulators (2610.09484, 2601.22607).
