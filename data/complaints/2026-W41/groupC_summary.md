## Complaints that appear in three or more papers

1. **The rollout stage dominates wall-clock and GPUs idle on the long tail of slow trajectories.** Learner or rollout GPUs wait for the slowest sample or group; reported idle fractions are 49.5% to 81% of a synchronous step. (2610.05935, 2602.17616, 2610.03286, 2609.35158, 2605.06534, 2608.22167, 2608.14635, 2605.22781)
2. **Asynchronous overlap buys throughput at the price of policy staleness that destabilizes or biases learning.** Stale rollouts produce heavy-tailed importance weights, force extra correction machinery, lose accuracy at fixed budget, or bias which trajectories survive. (2602.17616, 2610.05935, 2610.03286, 2605.22781)
3. **One container or micro-VM per environment is too expensive in memory, storage and startup, and memory, not CPU, is the hard cap on sandbox density.** Per-task images cost hundreds of GB; hugetlb-backed VM memory cannot be swapped; identical runtimes are duplicated across rollouts. (2602.11210, 2602.03419, 2609.32750, 2610.03286, 2604.28138, 2608.22167, 2506.11425)
4. **Full-state checkpoint, restore or branching of a sandbox is too slow (hundreds of ms to seconds, 4 s pauses on E2B) and does not scale under co-location, so mid-trajectory rollback and tree search are unavailable.** (2605.22781, 2604.28138, 2608.14635, 2602.11767, 2602.03419)
5. **Environment and tool failures corrupt training rather than just slowing it.** Non-idempotent actions cannot be safely replayed, a 10% tool-failure rate starves the reward signal, and one environment crash takes down a co-located training job. (2608.14635, 2603.21972, 2606.04484, 2604.28138)
6. **Weight synchronization to rollout engines across clusters is slow and tied to fixed process groups that cannot handle GPUs joining or leaving.** Full-checkpoint transfer takes minutes to 87.5 minutes; collectives couple membership and fail on timeouts. (2610.08430, 2605.06534, 2609.35158)
7. **Learned or hand-crafted scoring signals for partial trajectories or outcomes are unreliable or get exploited.** Policies hack learned reward models; prefix scores are noisy early; progress heuristics are task-specific. (2602.03419, 2606.04484, 2608.08020, 2602.11767, 2603.21972)
8. **KV-cache pressure in multi-turn rollouts: suspended trajectories get evicted and recomputed, long contexts saturate cache and queue requests.** (2610.03286, 2608.08020, 2605.06534, 2608.14635)
9. **Results shown only on models up to 7B to 8B, dense architectures, or simulated and toy environments; frontier-scale, MoE, FP8 and real-world settings untested.** (2603.21972, 2602.11767, 2602.17616, 2609.32750, 2608.22167)
10. **Mechanisms are tied to one isolation backend (Linux namespaces, Docker overlay and CRIU, or micro-VMs) and do not transfer to the others.** (2602.11210, 2608.14635, 2610.03286, 2604.28138)
