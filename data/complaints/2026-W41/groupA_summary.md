## Complaints that appear in three or more papers

1. **One grade for a whole trajectory cannot say which step mattered.** GRPO's uniform advantage punishes good steps in failed runs and rewards bad steps in lucky ones. (2609.20004, 2607.16257, 2603.08754, 2512.17008, 2604.11037, 2610.00388, 2608.01667, 2604.10701, 2605.13217, 2605.00347)
2. **Groups where every rollout passes or every rollout fails give zero learning signal**, and on hard agent tasks that is a large share of compute. (2607.14171, 2605.05112, 2502.12272, 2609.36058, 2607.16257, 2512.18552)
3. **A critic as large as the policy roughly doubles memory and compute and is fragile**, and no paper has run the compute-matched comparison that would settle whether it is worth it. (2605.07579, 2605.00347, 2603.08754, 2608.01667, 2609.36058, 2608.16739, 2604.10701, 2410.01679, 2609.36802)
4. **Step-level credit from forking or resetting needs cheap, deterministic state restore**, which many environments (live services, robots, flaky tests) do not provide, and fork cost can eat the gain. (2607.14171, 2609.33875, 2410.01679, 2607.26417, 2605.05112, 2609.11061)
5. **GRPO becomes unstable or collapses as the horizon grows**, and the usual knobs only delay it. (2607.16257, 2512.17008, 2605.00347, 2609.36802, 2512.18552)
6. **A single baseline from the initial state is misaligned with how the odds change mid-episode**, making the baseline worse the longer the task. (2607.16257, 2603.08754, 2512.17008, 2609.20004)
7. **Methods that ask the model to judge its own steps (hindsight re-scoring, reflection, LLM judges) only work if the model is strong enough, and may mostly measure fluency.** (2609.36864, 2603.08754, 2608.19760, 2512.18552)
8. **Sharing value estimates across states needs a notion of "same state", and exact matching fails outside deterministic text environments.** (2605.13217, 2604.11037, 2607.16257, 2610.00388)
9. **Dense or process rewards get hacked**: shaped rewards rise while task success falls, agents subvert test harnesses, and visible oracles invite overfitting. (2607.04713, 2609.33875, 2512.18552, 2609.11061)
10. **Published comparisons are not compute- or dose-matched**, so reported gains may measure how much training happened rather than the method. (2608.16739, 2609.36058, 2609.11061, 2608.19760, 2609.33875)
11. **Experiments stop at 7-14B and one or two benchmarks** because large-scale RL is unaffordable for the authors, so scaling behavior is unknown. (2511.14846, 2607.04713, 2608.16739, 2402.19446, 2605.13217, 2512.17008, 2604.11037, 2608.19760, 2609.36058)
12. **When the policy almost never succeeds, every method that learns from successes stalls**: no contrast for GRPO, no hindsight set, no reference trajectory for curricula. (2402.19446, 2607.16257, 2610.00388, 2609.20004, 2607.26417, 2502.12272)
